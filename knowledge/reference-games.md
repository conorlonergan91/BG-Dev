# Landmark Reference Games

Format: **Game** — Designer(s), year — what it's known for. Use these as touchstones when framing a
concept ("it's *Wingspan* meets *Root*") and for studying how respected designs solve problems.

## Gateway / mass-market classics

- **Catan** — Klaus Teuber, 1995 — launched the Euro hobby in the West; trading, resource dice, modular board. SdJ 1995.
- **Carcassonne** — Klaus-Jürgen Wrede, 2000 — tile-laying + meeple area majority; origin of the meeple. SdJ 2001.
- **Ticket to Ride** — Alan R. Moon, 2004 — set collection + route building; the model gateway game. SdJ 2004.
- **Pandemic** — Matt Leacock, 2008 — defined modern cooperative design.
- **Dominion** — Donald X. Vaccarino, 2008 — invented deck-building. SdJ 2009.
- **7 Wonders** — Antoine Bauza, 2010 — simultaneous card drafting; scales to 7 with almost no downtime. Inaugural Kennerspiel 2011.
- **Splendor** — Marc André, 2014 — the clean engine-building gateway.
- **Azul** — Michael Kiesling, 2017 — tactile tile-drafting/pattern building. SdJ 2018.

## Modern hobbyist heavyweights (BGG top tier)

- **Brass: Birmingham** — Wallace / Roxley redev, 2018 — long-running **BGG #1**; industrial-era network + economy.
- **Ark Nova** — Mathias Wigge, 2021 — card-driven zoo-building tableau + hex area; a "point salad" showcase; BGG top 2–3.
- **Gloomhaven** (2017) & **Frosthaven** (2022) — Isaac Childres — enormous legacy-lite tactical campaign crawlers; card-driven, no-dice combat.
- **Terraforming Mars** — Jacob Fryxelius, 2016 — card engine + tableau + shared-board terraforming; enduring top-10.
- **Twilight Imperium: 4th Ed.** — Petersen / Beltrami, 2017 — the definitive epic 4X negotiation game.
- **Spirit Island** — R. Eric Reuss, 2017 — heaviest mainstream co-op; asymmetric anti-colonial theme; strong solo.
- **Scythe** — Jamey Stegmaier, 2016 — asymmetric factions, engine + area presence, low-conflict Euro-thematic; famous table presence.
- **Root** — Cole Wehrle / Leder Games, 2018 — deeply **asymmetric** woodland wargame (COIN-derived); each faction plays by different rules.
- **Dune: Imperium** (2020) & **Uprising** (2023) — Paul Dennen — **deck-building + worker placement** hybrid; both BGG top 10.
- **Wingspan** — Elizabeth Hargrave, 2019 — bird engine-builder; crossover hit; Kennerspiel 2019; broadened the hobby's audience and designer base.
- **Everdell** — James A. Wilson, 2018 — worker placement + tableau engine; renowned table presence (3-D tree).
- **Lost Ruins of Arnak** — Elwen & Mín, 2020 — deck-building + worker placement + exploration.
- **Cascadia** — Randy Flynn, 2021 — light tile + token pattern puzzle; SdJ 2022; strong solo.
- **Pandemic Legacy: Season 1** — Leacock / Daviau, 2015 — proved the legacy format; long-time BGG top 5.

## Party / lighter award-winners

- **Codenames** — Vlaada Chvátil, 2015 — word-association team party game. SdJ 2016.
- **Dixit** — Jean-Louis Roubira, 2008 — evocative-art storytelling. SdJ 2010.
- **The Crew** — Thomas Sing, 2019 — cooperative trick-taking. Kennerspiel 2020.

## Two-player & co-op touchstones (studio focus — see `designer-profile.md`)

- **Splendor Duel** — Marc André / Bruno Cathala, 2022 — 2p engine/gem **race** on a shared board with a
  tug-of-war pull; multiple win paths. Elegant duel.
- **7 Wonders Duel** — Bauza & Bombela, 2015 — the model 2p **tug-of-war** (military + science tracks) +
  card-pyramid draft.
- **The Lord of the Rings: Duel for Middle-earth** — Reiner Knizia / Repos, 2024 — 2p tug-of-war in the
  7WD lineage; asymmetric pull toward opposing win conditions.
- **Patchwork** — Uwe Rosenberg, 2014 — 2p **spatial** quilt-building on a shared time track; cozy, tactile.
- **Jaipur** / **Watergate** / **The Fox in the Forest** — other sharp 2p duels (market set-collection /
  asymmetric tug-of-war / trick-taking).
- **Hanabi** — Antoine Bauza, 2010 — **constrained-communication** co-op (SdJ 2013); you can't see your
  own hand and may give only coded hints. The bottleneck *is* the game.
- **Sky Team** — Luc Rémond, 2023 — 2p co-op, **no table talk**, simultaneous dice placement landing a
  plane; SdJ 2024 (first 2p-only winner). Tense, atmospheric, clean.
- **SAIL** — 2p co-op, **no talking**, sailing/hand management — same constrained-comms DNA, cozy-tense.
- **The Crew** — Thomas Sing, 2019 — co-op trick-taking with limited signalling; Kennerspiel 2020.
- **Slay the Spire: The Board Game** — 2024 — co-op **deckbuilder** with a campaign/progression arc.

## Awards & ranking context (verify live)

- **Spiel des Jahres (SdJ)** rewards accessible, elegant family games; **Kennerspiel des Jahres**
  rewards heavier "connoisseur" games. Both drive large sales. **BGG rank** skews to the
  dedicated-hobbyist, heavier-game audience and is **not** a sales proxy.
- **2024:** SdJ — **Sky Team** (Luc Rémond; first two-player-only winner). Kennerspiel — **Daybreak**
  (Leacock & Menapace, climate co-op).
- **2025:** SdJ — **Bomb Busters** (Hisashi Hayashi). Kennerspiel — **Endeavor: Deep Sea**
  (de Visser & Gray).
- **BGG all-time top** is dominated by heavier Euros/hybrids: Brass: Birmingham (#1), Ark Nova,
  Pandemic Legacy S1, Gloomhaven, Dune: Imperium – Uprising / Dune: Imperium, Twilight Imperium 4E,
  War of the Ring 2E, Terraforming Mars, Star Wars: Rebellion.

> re-check with `bg-research` when it matters. Sources: spiel-des-jahres.de, boardgamegeek.com.
