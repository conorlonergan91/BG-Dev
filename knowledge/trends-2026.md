# Trends (2023–2026)

Current context for positioning a design. **Re-verify live** for a real launch — the market moves.

- **Solo gaming is a durable, growing pillar**, not a niche. A dedicated solo mode / Automa is now
  expected on mid-to-heavy releases. Co-op and legacy/campaign categories show strong projected growth.
- **Cozy / cute games surged** and dominate 2025–2026 slates — low-conflict, comforting, warm
  aesthetics (lineage of Wingspan, Cascadia, Everdell). "Cozy & Cute" is a tracked, high-performing
  crowdfunding sub-category.
- **Roll-and-write / flip-and-write** stayed strong — cheap to produce, low downtime, high replay
  (post-*Ganz schön clever*).
- **Legacy/campaign maturation and some fatigue.** After the Pandemic Legacy / Gloomhaven boom, the
  market cooled on destroy-your-components legacy toward **replayable "legacy-lite" / campaign** and
  reset-friendly designs (time commitment, shelf cost, one-and-done concerns). Big campaigns still
  sell, but the novelty premium narrowed.
- **Digital-first prototyping is mainstream.** Tabletop Simulator, Tabletopia, and Screentop are core
  to iteration, remote blind-testing, and crowdfunding demos — compressing the cycle and globalizing
  playtesting. *(This studio prioritizes a TTS-ready prototype early.)*
- **Crowdfunding platform shift.** Kickstarter is no longer the default: **Gamefound** captured much of
  the heavier-hobbyist segment (co-op, campaign, miniatures) and hosted a majority of 2024's
  top-funded tabletop campaigns; **BackerKit** grew as both a platform and the standard **pledge
  manager**. Kickstarter still leads for broader-appeal, cozy, medium-light titles. Native pledge
  managers, late pledges, and ongoing/subscription models are standard.
- **Market scale & pressures.** ~$12–13B market in 2025 and growing, but 2024–2026 brought **rising
  freight/manufacturing costs and tariff uncertainty** squeezing margins/shipping, plus debate over
  **AI-generated art** (backer/publisher backlash favoring human artists and "no-AI" pledges — a real
  positioning consideration).
- **Design zeitgeist.** Continued dominance of **Euro-thematic hybrids** and **point-salad** heavy
  games at the BGG top; strong appetite for **asymmetry** (post-Root); **card-driven "engine +
  tableau"** as the default hobbyist chassis (Ark Nova / Wingspan / Terraforming Mars lineage);
  broadening designer/player demographics (the "Wingspan effect").

**Positioning takeaways for a new design:**
- Plan a **solo mode** from the start if targeting mid/heavy hobbyists.
- A **free TTS/Tabletopia module** is now table stakes for a crowdfunding launch.
- Decide your **art stance** deliberately (human vs AI) — it affects backer trust.
- Pick the crowdfunding platform to match weight/audience (Gamefound = heavy/hobbyist; Kickstarter =
  broad/cozy/lighter).
- Budget conservatively for **freight/tariffs**.
