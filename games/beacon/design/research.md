# Beacon — Research (market & positioning)

*Output of the `bg-research` stage. Baseline from `knowledge/`; verify comps live before a real launch.*

## The space: 2-player, ~20-min, light-medium "duel" fillers

A crowded but evergreen category — couples/friends want a quick thinky duel. The bar is set by a few
beloved titles, so differentiation must come from a **distinct core-tension hook**, not just theme
(mechanics aren't protectable — see `publishing-ip.md`).

## Comparables

| Game | What it nails | Weakness / opening | Weight · time |
|------|---------------|--------------------|---------------|
| **The Fox in the Forest** | Trick-taking as a 2p duel; elegant | Trick-taking is a crowded core; theme thin | ~1.6 · 30m |
| **Air, Land & Sea** | Tiny tug-of-war blind-ish commitment; tense | Very abstract; minimal theme/table presence | ~1.7 · 20m |
| **Jaipur** | Set collection + market tension; snappy | No shared-track drama; solved-feeling for some | ~1.5 · 30m |
| **7 Wonders Duel** | Tug-of-war tracks (military/science) + draft | Heavier/longer; not a filler | ~2.2 · 30m |
| **Patchwork** | Spatial + time track; cozy | Spatial puzzle, not a bluff/commit game | ~1.6 · 30m |
| **The Crew (2p)** | Co-op trick-taking; huge hit | Co-op, not competitive; different itch | ~2.0 · 20m |

## The gap
Few light 2p fillers combine **simultaneous blind commitment** (the fun of a standoff) with a **visible
tug-of-war track that also feeds a catch-up loop**. Air, Land & Sea is closest but stays fully abstract
and card-only. **Beacon's opening:** the same taut blind-commit tension, plus a **cozy, legible
nautical hook** and a **Tide track that both scores and re-arms the loser** — more warmth and table
presence than the abstract incumbents, still a 20-minute filler.

## Positioning recommendation
- **Weight ~1.8, 2 players, ~20 min, price band €/£/$15–20** (small-box card game).
- **Platform:** Screentop.gg for playtesting; if it graduates, a small-box print run (POD cards) and a
  Kickstarter (broad/cozy audience fits KS better than Gamefound) — see `trends-2026.md`.
- **Audience:** couples, café/travel players, fans of Fox in the Forest / Air, Land & Sea / Jaipur.

## Risks & differentiators
- **Clone perception vs Air, Land & Sea** — differentiate on the Tide-reclaim loop, set-collection
  scoring, and cozy identity (art/name/get-up are the protectable differentiators).
- **Swinginess** — blind commit can feel random; the Tide track + 3-Night structure must reward reading
  your opponent over luck (a core playtest question).
