# Beacon — Rules (draft v0.1)

*Output of the `bg-developer` stage. Written teach-in-play-order per `knowledge/process-and-playtesting.md`.
Blind-test this rulebook, not just the game.*

## The story
Two lighthouse keepers, one treacherous coast. Across **three nights** you read the fog, work the
**Tide**, and guide the richest ships into **your** harbour. Most valuable harbour after the third
night wins.

## Components
- **24 Ship cards** — three suits (⚓ Cargo, ☺ Passenger, ⚔ Naval), values **1–4** (two of each per suit).
- **2 sets of 6 Signal cards** (values **0–5**) — one set each.
- **Tide track** (7 spaces) + **1 Tide marker**.
- **First-Keeper token**.

## Goal
Score the most **Victory Points** after 3 Nights, from the ships in your Harbour, set bonuses, and
Tide bonuses.

## Setup
1. Shuffle the 24 Ships; deal three face-down **Night decks** of 8.
2. Put the **Tide marker** on the centre space.
3. Each player takes a **set of 6 Signals** to hand.
4. Give the **First-Keeper token** to whoever last saw the sea (or randomly).

## Playing a Night (do this three times)
1. **Reveal** the top 2 ships of this Night's deck to the **Channel**.
2. Play **4 rounds**:
   a. Both keepers secretly pick **one Signal** and reveal together.
   b. **Higher value wins the beam:** move the **Tide one space toward you**. **But your opponent (who
      showed the dimmer light) picks a Channel ship first** — a dim light snatches the cargo in front of
      it while your bright beam steers the Tide. You take the ship they leave.
      *Tie:* the Tide stays; the **First Keeper** chooses first, then the other.
   c. Set spent Signals aside. **Refill** the Channel back to 2 from this Night's deck.
   (You will commit 4 of your 6 Signals this Night and hold 2 back.)
3. **End of Night:**
   - **Tide bonus:** whoever's side the Tide is on scores **2 points per space from centre** (up to 6;
     centre = nobody). The harder you pushed the Tide, the bigger the payoff.
   - Return the Tide marker to **centre**; take all your Signals back to hand.
   - **The tide turns:** the keeper who **lost the Tide** (fewest ships if centred) takes the
     **First-Keeper token** for the next Night.

## Game end & scoring
After the third Night, total up:
- **Ship values** in your Harbour.
- **Convoy bonus:** +2 for each {Cargo + Passenger + Naval} set.
- **Run bonus:** +2 for each run of 3 consecutive values in one suit.
- **Tide bonuses** earned during play.

Most VP wins. Tie → most **Naval** ships; still tied → shared victory.

## Optional module — Fog (push-your-luck)
When revealing the Channel, place the top ship **face-down**. The beam winner may take it **sight
unseen** before the face-up one. *Adds a gamble; test after the base game feels right.*

## Player aid (quick reference)
> Reveal 2 → both commit a Signal → **higher = Tide step toward you; *lower* picks a ship first** → refill → ×4 rounds →
> Night end: Tide side scores 2×spaces-from-centre, reset, loser becomes First Keeper. After Night 3:
> score ships + convoys + runs + Tide.
