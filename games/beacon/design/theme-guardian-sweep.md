Beacon's felt identity is in good shape and still fits its mechanics. The restraint rung

## 2. Resonance check — signature twist and core mechanics

Reviewed each core mechanic against what changed since the last identity pass (Tide scaling v0.3, the
pending Run-bonus cut, the critic's open-hand/solvability flag).

- **Signature twist — "the tide turns" — INTEGRATED (unchanged, strongest link).** The loser of the
  Tide taking First-Keeper next Night still reads as the sea literally handing advantage back to the
  trailing keeper. The v0.3 tune (Tide scores 2×spaces-from-centre, was flat 3) is a *numeric* change,
  not a fiction change — and if anything it **strengthens** the fiction: a tide that has run further is
  worth more. No re-skin needed.
- **Blind commit = the fog — INTEGRATED.** Committing your Signal before seeing your rival's is the fog.
  *Watch-item (from `design/critique.md`):* the critic's concern that open hands + a tiny depleting
  Signal set may make the read solvable in silence is a play-health issue, but it has a theme edge — if
  the read becomes deterministic, the "fog" fiction quietly stops being true. The proposed fix (a
  hidden-hand variant Night) would **tighten** the fog fiction, not strain it. Good news; log the
  re-theme note if/when that variant lands. No action for `bg-theme` now.
- **Signal values 0–5, hold 2 of 6 = the beam's reach — INTEGRATED.** Husbanding a limited light. The
  regenerated lamp/beam emblem (2026-08-06) reinforced this cleanly.
- **Set collection (Convoy + Run) — MOSTLY INTEGRATED; Run is the weakest name.** "Convoy" (one of each
  ship type sheltered under your light) is evocative and earns its keep. "Run of 3 consecutive values in
  one suit" is the one bonus nobody *feels* — it's a maths pattern, not a picture. Elegance already flags
  cutting Run as input-luck noise. **From a theme lens, cutting Run is a net positive**: it removes the
  layer's one pasted-on element and leaves the more evocative Convoy carrying it. Endorse the cut.

## 4. Naming & voice — coherent

Signal · Tide · Night · Channel · First-Keeper · Convoy all share one register and each teaches its rule.
Ship names are on-world and characterful. Voice (keeper's-log warmth) is consistent. No clearance items

## 5. The one note worth surfacing — the Naval suit fits the game least

This is the single spot where a layer points a slightly different way from the rest:

- **Against the wealth fantasy:** the whole pitch is "guide the **richest ships / fat cargo runs** into
  your harbour." Cargo = wealth (obvious). Passenger = fares (fine). **Naval warships are not cargo
  wealth** — the fiction never quite says *why* guiding a warship into your port scores you VP.
- **Against the locked cozy-warm tone:** the Naval flavour is overtly martial and menacing — *"Nothing
  outguns her," "Feared from cape to cape," "Dread Aurora," "Brine Serpent," "colossal ship of the
  line."* That reads grim/cold in a game whose tone is explicitly "tense but warm... no cruelty, never
  grim... a duel you play by a fire." It's the one place icon + flavour lean harder than the stated vibe
  (the ⚔ crossed-swords suit icon is the tell). The `most Naval ships` tie-break leans on this suit too.

It's a **light** dissonance in an otherwise integrated theme — acceptable as-is for a filler, but this
is the moment (pre-first-human-playtest, art still placeholder) when a re-skin costs nothing and after
art it costs everything.

## Sensitivity & representation check — PASSES TRIVIALLY

## Anti-drift / anti-restriction — nothing frozen, nothing muddling

## Decision for the human (the one identity call — optional)

**Should the third ship suit stay martial, or warm up to match the cozy-wealth fantasy?** My pick: **B.**

- **A — Keep Naval/⚔ as-is.** Accept it as light dressing; a coastal trio can include a navy. Zero effort.
  Cost: the one persistent tone/resonance soft-spot stays.
- **B — Re-skin the third suit to fit the wealth-and-warmth fantasy** (my pick). Swap warships for
  something a keeper genuinely profits from guiding home — e.g. **Fishing** (⚓/net motif; "the catch")
  or **Traders/Merchant**. Retheme the eight flavour lines + icon; tie-break becomes "most [new suit]."
  Fixes both the *why does this score* gap and the tone clash in one move, while art is still placeholder
  and free to change. Mechanics untouched (still three suits, values 1–4).
- **C — Keep Naval but re-fiction why it scores and soften the icon.** Navies pay keepers an *escort fee*
  for safe passage; swap ⚔ for an escort/anchor-and-star motif and warm the flavour lines. Keeps the
  martial silhouette but pulls it back toward the tone. Lightest change, partial fix.

Recommendation: **B** — cleanest resonance, best tone fit, and the timing (pre-art, pre-playtest) makes
it nearly free. If the human would rather not touch components before the first human session, **C** is
the low-risk holding move and **A** is a legitimate "it's a filler, leave it" call.
