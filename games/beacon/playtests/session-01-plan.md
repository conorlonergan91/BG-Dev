# Beacon — Playtest Session 1 (PLAN)

*Output of the `bg-playtest` stage. Run on Screentop.gg with your friend, then log results in a copy of
`templates/playtest-log.md`.*

## Hypothesis / question
**Does the blind Signal commit reward reading your opponent (skill), or does it feel random over 3
Nights?** (This is the game's make-or-break question — see design open question #1.)

## Secondary questions
- Is "commit 4, hold 2 of 6" the right signal economy, or does it feel starved / loose? (Q2)
- Do the convoy/run set bonuses change how you pick ships, or are they ignored? (Q3)

## What to observe
- **Fun spikes** — when did a reveal get a laugh or a groan? (Those moments are the game.)
- **Dead/obvious turns** — any round where the Signal choice felt forced.
- **Runaway leader** — did the Tide bonus + initiative handoff actually keep it close?
- **Confusion points** — every rules question is a rulebook bug (log the exact wording).
- **Metrics** — total time, final scores, how often each Signal value got committed, how often the
  Tide bonus swung the game.

## Facilitation
- **Full test** (you facilitate) for session 1 — you'll be probing the core question. Save the **blind
  test** (rules-only, no help) for session 3+.
- Play **best of two** so both players sit as First Keeper.
- Don't explain optimal play; note when a player *discovers* the "throw a low Signal to bait the 5" line
  — that discovery is the sign the depth is real.

## Data to capture
Use `templates/playtest-log.md`. Especially: a tally of committed Signal values, and a note on each
Night's Tide outcome and margin.

## Pre-registered "next change" branches
- If it feels **too random** → add a small **memory/info** lever (e.g. reveal 1 upcoming ship) or let a
  loser peek before committing. Test one only.
- If **signal economy feels loose** → try commit 5 of 7.
- If **set bonuses ignored** → cut the run bonus, keep convoys; re-test.

## Success criteria
Players want to immediately play again, and at least one "I should have read that" moment per game.
