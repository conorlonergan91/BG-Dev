**BEACON**
*Two keepers. One coast. Read the fog before your rival does.*

### The pitch
*The Fox in the Forest* meets *Air, Land & Sea*. Each night, ships slip through the Channel and your
lighthouse can only guide so many. Commit your signal **before** you see your rival's — win the beam to
swing the **Tide** and claim the fattest cargo. But every night the tide turns, handing the trailing
keeper the advantage. A tense, cozy 20-minute duel with no dice and no luck after you've chosen.

### What makes it special
- **Pure read-your-opponent tension** — blind, simultaneous, all skill after the reveal.
- **The Tide turns** — a built-in comeback lever keeps both keepers in it to the last night.
- **Small box, big decisions** — 36 cards, a track, and one gorgeous nautical mood.
- **Warmer than the abstracts it rivals** — a coast you actually want to spend an evening on.

### In the box (at a glance)
24 Ship cards · 12 Signal cards · Tide track + marker · First-Keeper token · rules & player aid.

### Comparables
*The Fox in the Forest*, *Air, Land & Sea*, *Jaipur* — Beacon adds a scoring tug-of-war with a
catch-up loop and a cozier identity.
