# Beacon — IP & Compliance Quick Plan (IE / UK / US)

*Output of the `bg-publishing` stage. **General information, not legal advice** — confirm registrations,
contracts, and safety testing with a qualified solicitor/attorney and an accredited lab per market.
Full background: `knowledge/publishing-ip.md`.*

## Where the protectable value is
Beacon's **mechanics aren't protectable** (blind selection + tug-of-war are free for anyone). The value
to protect is the **name "Beacon", the logo, the artwork, the rulebook text, and the box get-up**. It's
also a **crowded 2p-duel space**, so a distinctive cozy-nautical identity is both the marketing edge and
the legal moat.

## Sequenced actions

**Now (pre-art, cheap/free):**
- [ ] **Clear the name.** "Beacon" is a common word — run a **free clearance search** in class 28
      (IPOI/EUIPO/UKIPO/USPTO databases) + BGG + Steam/app stores + domain/socials. **High risk of
      conflict** on a plain word like this → consider a distinctive full title (e.g. *"Beacon: Keepers
      of the Coast"*) to clear more easily. **Decision needed** (see below).
- [ ] Keep dated originals of all design files (automatic copyright in IE/UK; evidence of authorship).

**When commissioning art:**
- [ ] Every artist signs a **written copyright assignment** (UK/IE freelancers own by default even when
      paid; US shouldn't rely on work-for-hire alone). Address moral rights + originality warranty.
- [ ] Confirm **font and asset licences** allow commercial print use. Committed to **no AI art** (backer
      trust + cleaner US copyright).

**Near launch:**
- [ ] **Trademark** the final name/logo in **class 28**: **EUIPO** if selling EU-wide (covers Ireland;
      ~€850) or **IPOI** if IE-only (~€247); add **UKIPO** (~£205) and **USPTO** (~$350/class); use the
      **Madrid Protocol** once going multi-territory.
- [ ] **US copyright:** register the **rulebook + artwork** at/near publication (within 3 months) to
      unlock the right to sue + statutory damages.

**Before selling a physical copy (mandatory if marketed to under-14s — a card duel likely is family):**
- [ ] **EU/IE:** Toy Safety Directive + **EN 71** testing → **Declaration of Conformity** + technical
      file → **CE mark**.
- [ ] **UK:** CE accepted indefinitely (or UKCA); equivalent EN 71.
- [ ] **US:** **ASTM F963 / CPSIA** third-party testing → **Children's Product Certificate** + tracking
      label.
- [ ] **Age grading** + small-parts note on the box (cards only = low choking risk, but confirm with the
      lab). **GS1 EAN/UPC** only if going to retail/distribution.

## Decision for the human
**Name strategy:** (A) keep the evocative-but-risky *"Beacon"* and budget for a harder clearance; (B) go
with a distinctive full title like *"Beacon: Keepers of the Coast"* to clear class 28 more easily
(recommended); or (C) rename entirely. This should be settled **before** any art spend.
