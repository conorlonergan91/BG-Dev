## Decisions currently in force (V7-M) — the load-bearing current rules

*A skim of the rules the game runs on NOW (merged in PR #13, sim-validated N=400). Every bullet traces to
a full entry in the log below (V7 era) and to `game-bible.md` / `components/*.csv`. This is a summary, not a
new decision.*

- **Multiplayer 3–6, ~30–45 min.** The 2-player duel is **dropped** as a design target (V7 pivot, 2026-08-09); everyone starts in Taipei (North).
- **Followers are meeples + area-majority.** Hype is committed **follower meeples** piled ON a stall; you win a vendor (**Sign Up**) by holding the **biggest crowd** (strict plurality — a tie blocks). Pool = **6** meeples; the Influencer 網紅 owns **8**. The **Followers-max dial** caps committed followers (grows on Travel + events; a lost war drops it).
- **Vendors are the clock (depletion) — a DAY engine, a NIGHT payoff.** The finite vendor pools ARE the clock: **18 day engine-seeds → 黃昏 DUSK**, **12 night trophies (11 unique + 1 duplicate) → 末班車 LAST TRAIN**. DAY = cheap, varied low-VP seeds you claim to build a region-discount engine; NIGHT = scarce, expensive high-VP trophies the engine lets you afford. The Booking decks are NOT the clock; ingredient + event decks reshuffle.
- **Closing-Time die (關門) — the forcing function, PER-PHASE cadence.** ONE custom d6 `[N,C,S,E,BLANK,BLANK]` (the game's only die) — only how *often* it rolls differs by phase: **DAY** rolls after **L** rounds (**L=2 at 3p, L=3 at 4–6p** — a gentle cadence that protects day-engine formation); **NIGHT** rolls at the **end of every round** ("closing time", draining the night pool to the last train). A rolled region face closes that region's oldest face-up stall — contested or not — followers go **home to boards, kept, no penalty**; BLANK = a reprieve.
- **Multi-ingredient vendor recipes + a min-followers floor + the no-icon night.** Every vendor is a multi-ingredient, mostly cross-region dish carrying two printed numbers: **`min_followers`** (crowd-floor = star tier 1/2/3) and **`vp`**. **Day food seeds carry their region's discount icon (they ARE the engine); the day drinks and ALL night vendors — ★ apex included — carry NO icon** (trophies are prizes, not engine — this kills the old mid-vendor hoarder double-dip).
- **TWO-TRACK VP scoring.** Score = the **`vp` on every won vendor (0–11 — day seeds low 1–2, night trophies high 5–11)** + the **full value of every completed tour (Booking, 3–11)**. Tours carry the bulk of a winner's score (booking-share ~67–75% vs vendor-share ~25–33%); the high-VP night trophies are the vendor payoff but a vendor-only hoard is structurally dominated (§14 invariant: `vp = ingredient_cost + min_followers − 2`). Cancelled bookings = **flat −1** each. *(⚠ `rules-draft.md` §1/§9 + `names.md` §3 still read "tickets-only / vendors never score / cancel = −½ value" — a stale-rulebook contradiction flagged to bg-rulebook / bg-continuity / bg-design; the sim, `vendors.csv` vp column, and §14 are two-track.)*
- **Bookings (開團) + Pot-Luck.** Tour cards, hand-limit **3**, cancel-to-claim; three kinds **⟳ any-time / ☀ day / ☾ night**. Book from the face-up row OR **Pot-Luck (隨緣開團)** — decline the row, draw the deck's top blind and keep it.
- **Six asymmetric characters (V7-M kits, locked).** Old Councillor 里長伯 (home-gated ingredient DRAW), Homebody 媽寶 (sole Travel-sourcer), Big Eater 大胃王 (keep-3 + greedy-4th; hazards fire twice; **she/her**), Influencer 網紅 (deep **8**-meeple pool; goes last), Big Boss 老大 (**margin dial N=1** — wins ties), Globetrotter 阿嬤 (Travel-anywhere + Scheduled-Maintenance-immune; 1 follower/hop). *(V7-M reworks the Old Councillor from the old −1/+1 discount to a home-gated draw, and the Big Eater/Influencer/Big Boss kits from their v7-plan/names.md shapes — `characters.csv` is canon; balance reopened → re-sim.)*
- **Turn = ONE action:** Market Run / Convenience Store / Travel / Promote / Sign Up / Book. Larder cap **7**; Greedy Grab (keep 1 extra → return 1 follower to supply).
- **MONO 6-type ingredient taxonomy ships:** 肉 Meat · 茶 Tea · 海鮮 Seafood · 糧 Pantry · 糖 Sugar · 菜 Vegetables (each card art is a MIX). The **8-type split is PARKED** — a non-destructive, flag-gated branch (`SPLIT_TAXONOMY`, default OFF; `*-split.csv`).
- **Events — VERB · SCOPE · DURATION framework.** Scopes ⦿ self · ✦ rival · ⬢ region · ➤ neighbour · ▦ board; hazards tax-not-destroy; rival-facing ✦ auto-targets the leader, magnitude 1, a small minority. **Scheduled Maintenance (排班保養)** is the island-wide HSR-down beat (阿嬤 immune).
- **Trending (爆紅) vs ★ apex — distinct.** Trending = the dynamic, event-dropped going-viral marker (winning a Trending stall grants +1 Followers-max); the printed **★ apex** is the permanent marquee. **Rave Reviews (好評)** = per-player milestone boons at your 2nd & 4th completed tour.
- **Complexity:** weight target **~2.2 / ceiling ~2.5**; rulebook cap **2280** words.
- **Sensitivity rails (binding).** Traditional characters only, secular, dignified locals, faithful dishes; **Big Boss 老大**, **Old Councillor 里長伯**, and the Indigenous items (**E01 竹筒飯, E04 石板烤肉**) are consultant-gated; a **paid Taiwanese cultural + language consultant is required before art/print**. No AI final art (AI only for labelled/gitignored playtest mock-ups).

Accepted trade (table watch): night can end by the die closing trophies faster than players claim them → a couple fewer trophies get bought — a game that ends beats one that never does. Authority: `map.json` `vendor_clock.track_length` (day) + `track_length_night` (night); sim `playtests/sims/sweep-two-dice-N400-confirm.txt`. *(Rejected: a second custom NIGHT die — face harshness barely moved the stall, so a gentler/harsher die is neither needed nor helpful.)* (game-bible.md, decision-log.md)
