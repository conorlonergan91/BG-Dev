# The Shared Table Map — illustration brief (V7)

> **Briefs only. NO AI final art** (studio rail — `designer-profile.md`). Direction for a **human
> illustrator**; the board renders as a functional, labelled layout (`build_board.py --type regionmap`,
> two states via `--phase day|night`) until real art lands. This brief names the figure codes the board's
> composition RESERVES art space for, so the illustrated centrepiece drops onto the *same* functional
> skeleton (region diamond, HSR spine, Booking deck + row, vendor slots) without moving a single play
> position.
>
> **What this board is (read first — V7):** the **neutral shared table** for **3–6 players**. There is
> **NO across-the-table 2-player mirror** — the board reads **upright from any seat** (one orientation, no
> mirror-rotated edges). The 4-region **diamond** is the neutral shared map: **北 NORTH top · 南 SOUTH
> bottom** sit on the vertical **HSR spine** (a drawn N↔S double-rail down the centre, carrying the **停
> Scheduled-Maintenance** reminder slot); **中 CENTRAL · 東 EAST** flank left and right. Each region has its
> own **vendor slots** (a face-down region deck + face-up slots, count scaling with players). The clock is
> **DECK-DRIVEN**, not a printed track (see below): a shared central **開團 Booking** deck feeds a **face-up
> Booking row** — the sole prize row. **Per-player instruments are NOT on this board** — the **Followers
> dial** and the **成團 COMPLETED / 取消 CANCELLED** Booking piles live on each **character board**.
>
> **The clock is the DECK, not a track (V7 — replaces the old numbered vendor-claims clock).** There is **no
> printed claims-clock rail**. The day→night→end beat is carried entirely by two decks and two seeded
> **boundary cards**: the **DAY 開團 Booking deck** runs until it exhausts → the seeded **黃昏 DUSK** boundary
> card flips the table to night → the **NIGHT 開團 Booking deck** runs until *it* exhausts → the seeded **末班車
> LAST TRAIN** boundary card ends the game. So the board reserves a **central Booking-deck zone + a face-up
> Booking row**, NOT a track of numbered cells. The day/night STATE reads from the **chrome flip** (warm
> gold day → lantern-neon night) plus which 開團 deck is face-up — never from a clock position.
>
> **Shared direction:** the warm night-market palette (lantern vermilion `#D64545` / jade teal `#1F8A7A` /
> golden amber `#E6A930` / dusk indigo `#26324D` on bao-paper cream `#F7F1E3`); the day state = warm
> dawn/gold chrome, the night state = festive lantern-**neon** (lavender/magenta) — *neon glow, never a
> cold or red alarm register*. **Region identity colours stay warm in BOTH states** (only the chrome flips)
> — hold that. Secular only (no temple/deity/ghost-month motifs). Taiwan is the **beloved**, celebrated
> *with* not laughed *at*; the island is a living subject that pushes the agents around, never passive
> exotica to be catalogued.
>
> **Binding legibility rails (bg-infodesign — do NOT let art break these):** every region stays
> dual-coded **colour + emblem + 繁體** (▲北 / ◆中 / ●南 / ■東); the day→night beat must stay readable as
> **sun→moon + the active 開團 deck** (chrome + phase, never colour alone); illustration is a
> **backdrop/vignette behind** the functional zones — vendor card slots, region decks, the Booking deck +
> face-up Booking row, the HSR spine and the 停 slot must stay high-contrast on top of any art (drop a
> translucent scrim under text if a painted area would eat contrast). Art serves the at-a-glance read,
> never buries it.

## [FIG-MAP-ISLAND] — the Taiwan-island backdrop (behind the diamond)
- **Where:** the central diamond area behind the four region panels + the HSR spine (roughly the middle
  third of the board). A soft, stylised **island of Taiwan** motif tying the four regions into one place —
  a gentle relief map (Central mountains, the western plain, the east coast), not a literal atlas.
- **Tone:** a warm travel-poster / vintage-tourism-stamp feel (the passport/boarding-pass identity). Dusk
  light in the day state; lantern-neon shimmer at night.
- **Neutral / any-seat:** compose so it reads **upright from every seat around the table** — no top/bottom
  "hero" orientation, no text or scene that only one player reads the right way up.
- **Keep clear:** the HSR spine (a drawn double-rail runs N↔S), the **停 Scheduled-Maintenance** reminder
  slot on the spine, and the region adjacency lines must read on top — treat the island as a low-contrast
  underlay.

## [FIG-MAP-NORTH] · [FIG-MAP-CENTRAL] · [FIG-MAP-SOUTH] · [FIG-MAP-EAST] — the four region vignettes
- **Where:** a small illustrated vignette in each region panel's header/ground, behind its face-up vendor
  slots and its region deck. North is **top of the diamond**, South **bottom** (both on the HSR spine);
  Central and East flank the sides.
- **Direction (one signature scene each, dish-typed & generic — NEVER a real named business):**
  - **北 NORTH (Taipei / Meat 肉 signature · vermilion ▲):** a capital night-market lane, steam off a
    pork-bun cart; this panel is the **common START (台北 Taipei)** for every agent — carry a subtle
    "head-office / departure" cue.
  - **中 CENTRAL (Tea 茶 signature · jade ◆):** tea terraces + a 手搖飲 hand-shaken-drink stand; the region
    that *exports* tea.
  - **南 SOUTH (Seafood 海鮮 signature · amber ●):** a Tainan/Kaohsiung quayside, oyster omelette on the
    griddle.
  - **東 EAST (Pantry 糧 signature · indigo ■):** the Hualien–Taitung coast/valley, rice + bamboo-tube fare.
    **Indigenous-forward — the paid Taiwanese/Indigenous consultant sign-off binds here** (dish, naming,
    framing) BEFORE art; warm, specific, dignified, never exotic-punchline; **no tribal iconography** in any
    interim placeholder.
- **Keep clear:** the region header banner (emblem + name + 繁體), the START·台北 pill on North, the region
  deck footprint, and the face-up vendor-slot footprints.

## [FIG-MAP-FRAME] — the night-market border (carries the day/night chrome)
- **Where:** the outer frame / margins of the whole board.
- **Direction:** a string-of-lanterns / hand-painted stall-signage border that frames the table as a
  night-market street. This border is the **primary day/night STATE cue now that the clock is deck-driven**:
  warm **gold bulbs + a rising sun** in the day state, glowing **lantern-neon + a moon** in the night state
  (the sun→moon read the legibility rail asks for). Warm in both states; only the chrome flips. This is the
  piece that makes the board a *stylish centrepiece* rather than a schematic.
- **Neutral / any-seat:** a continuous border with no single "front" — every seat sees the same warmth.
- **Keep clear:** the title band, the central Booking deck + face-up Booking row, and the region panels.

> **Retired figure code:** the old **[FIG-MAP-CLOCK]** (the numbered day→night clock rails on both long
> edges) is **GONE** — V7 has no printed claims-clock track. There is nothing to paint for the clock; the
> beat is the two 開團 decks + the 黃昏 DUSK / 末班車 LAST TRAIN boundary cards, and the day/night state rides on
> the frame + island chrome (`FIG-MAP-FRAME` / `FIG-MAP-ISLAND`).
