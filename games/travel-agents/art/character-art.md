# Travel Agents: Taiwan Food Tour — Character-Board Art Briefs (six characters)

Illustrator briefs for the six character-board portraits (the `[FIG-CHAR-<slug>]` slot on each board — a
passport / boarding-pass framed portrait). **Every brief below inherits `art/art-style.md`** — the CD's
2026-08 hand: **warm, hand-painted Studio-Ghibli-inspired anime** (Taiwan street-food travel-agent theme, a
wacky over-the-top food-tour race); soft painterly light, gentle organic outlines, luminous colour, the
night-market palette warm-day→neon-night; **characters caricatured with EXAGGERATED BODY FEATURES** and big
expressive faces; **warm, dignified, charming — never ethnic caricature or mockery**; deliberately NOT
photoreal and NOT a flat "AI/ChatGPT cartoon". Ghibli = the FEEL only, never a copied frame.
**Human-illustrated final art; AI for labelled mock-ups only.** A **paid Taiwanese cultural consultant**
reviews the cast before final art (watch-items: the Big Boss's warmth, the Globetrotter's spryness).

Shared framing: each is a **single character, waist-up or full-figure**, mid-action, big expressive face, a
strong readable silhouette, filling the board's portrait frame. **NO background scenery — just the
character and their own props (what they hold, wear, or ride), on a plain flat background: a clean cut-out
for the card.** Lovable, expressive personalities, laughing *with* the world — painted in the **warm Ghibli
hand with each body CARICATURED / exaggerated for charm and instant readability** (a round belly, a tiny
bird-frame, a broad huggable build — pushed well past realistic, but warm, never mocking). **Crank the
ENERGY: this is a fast RACE-AND-BATTLE game. Each character's POSE should DRAMATIZE their game ability (NOT
a generic run) — dynamic, zany, slightly CHAOTIC and competitive, with motion/speed lines; urgency and
glee, warm but never mean.** **Best results: generate one new Ghibli hero first, then attach THAT as the
style reference (NOT the old flat cards) so the cast stays cohesive.**

## `[FIG-CHAR-globetrotter]` — The Globetrotter (A-má / 阿嬤) ⚑ the one to nail

**Who:** a **spry, sharp-eyed grandma** who has already zipped to the next market before anyone else — her
free elder travel-pass (敬老卡) is her superpower. **Age is her advantage, never her frailty.**

**Body (exaggerated Ghibli caricature):** a **tiny, bird-boned little granny** — a small rounded frame
leaning forward into the speed, big round spectacles, an over-large wild pouf of silver curls, dainty
quick hands. Small and unstoppable, never hunched or frail.

**Pose / energy (over-the-top, warm):** caught **mid-zip and loving it** — e.g. one foot up on a little
電動代步車 (mobility scooter) or an electric bike, or striding in fast with a wheelie shopping-trolley, a
triumphant "**beat you here again!**" grin and a knowing wink, her **悠遊卡 EasyCard held up between two
fingers like a winning card** — ideally the **senior 敬老悠遊卡** (the instantly-recognisable local
stored-value card, which doubles as her free-elder-travel hook; NOT a generic train/elder card). Silver hair (a neat perm or bun), bright practical clothes (a floral blouse, a visor or
sun-sleeves, comfy trainers), maybe a small crossbody bag stuffed with snacks she's already bought. She is
**quick, canny, delighted** — the granny who knows every shortcut and every vendor by name. **Push it
ZANY: the scooter popping a wheelie, curls and scarf flying, a manic gleeful grin, snacks flying from the basket.**

**Do:** upright, energetic, confident, twinkling; a sense of *motion* (speed lines, a scarf/bag mid-swish).
**No background scenery — just her, her scooter, the EasyCard and her snack bag, on a plain background.**
**Don't:** hunched, cane-dependent, passive, confused, or a butt-of-the-joke. Not a frail stereotype — she's
the one *winning the race*. (Consultant + blind-test watch: reads warm/spry, never mocking of elders.)

## `[FIG-CHAR-old-councillor]` — The Old Councillor (Lí-tiúⁿ-peh / 里長伯)

A **cheerful borough boss with real local clout**. **Body (exaggerated Ghibli caricature):** round,
portly, barrel-chested with a proud pot belly, short legs, a big jowly beaming face — folksy warmth in
every curve. **Ability-pose (Old Favours / home-turf king):** one
foot planted triumphantly up on a crate like claiming his patch, his **megaphone BLARING a rally**
("鄉親作伙來!"), free arm flung wide to gather his neighbours in — a comic, chest-out "this whole lane is
MINE!" pride; apron + lanyard/sash. **Dignified and folksy, never a pompous or corrupt-politician gag. No
background scenery — just him and his props.**

## `[FIG-CHAR-homebody]` — The Homebody (Ma-pó / 媽寶)

An **earnest young office worker**. **Body (exaggerated Ghibli caricature):** soft, round-shouldered,
slightly doughy and boyish, big round glasses, a neat mop of hair — a little small under the load he
carries. **Ability-pose (Mum's Packed Lunch / sole travel-sourcer):** staggering
under a comically **teetering tower of mum-packed lunchboxes (便當) and grocery bags** (spring onions and
drinks poking out, about to topple), tie flapping, **phone wedged against his shoulder** ("Ma — which stall
next?!"), glasses askew, a delighted-but-overloaded grin — impossibly over-provisioned wherever he goes.
Warm, self-teasing — **affectionate, not a mocking "mama's boy." No background scenery — just him and his props.**

## `[FIG-CHAR-big-eater]` — The Big Eater (Tuā-uī-ông / 大胃王)

A **SLIM, PETITE YOUNG WOMAN** — true to Taiwan's famous tiny-but-bottomless competitive eaters. **Body
(exaggerated Ghibli caricature):** PUSH THE CONTRAST — an impossibly tiny, delicate bird-frame with a wasp
waist, yet cheeks comically over-bulged mid-mouthful; the little-body / bottomless-appetite gap is the whole
joke. **Ability-pose (Bottomless / keep all + greedy 4th):** cheeks
bulging mid-enormous mouthful, one hand shovelling noodles with chopsticks while the OTHER **snatches a 4th
bowl** off a towering, wobbling stack of empties; a "挑戰極限" headband, sparkling delighted eyes,
sauce-splash motion. Joyful and celebratory of Taiwan's abundance — **never gross-out or shaming.**

## `[FIG-CHAR-influencer]` — The Influencer (Bāng-âng / 網紅)

A **stylish food blogger** framing the perfect bowl for the feed — phone/ring-light up, one eye on the shot,
trendy fit, a delighted grin because they *genuinely* love the food. **Body (exaggerated Ghibli
caricature):** tall, willowy, long-limbed and impossibly photogenic — an exaggeratedly poised, leggy
fashion-forward frame, big sparkling eyes. A little ring of hearts/📈 "going viral" sparkle. **Affectionate
wink at influencer culture, never sneering. No background scenery — just them and their props.**

## `[FIG-CHAR-big-boss]` — The Big Boss (Lāu-tuā / 老大) ⚑ #1 dignity watch

The **warm neighbourhood big boss** — a lovable fixer in a bomber jacket (a tiger patch + 老大), a big
warm cinema-charm laugh and a friendly thumbs-up, holding a takeaway bag of night-market food. **Body
(exaggerated Ghibli caricature):** a BIG, broad, huggable barrel of a man — huge shoulders, giant warm
hands, a big round beaming face; his size reads GENEROUS and jolly (a gentle giant), NEVER looming or
menacing. **Solo — no
stall-owner, no background scenery, just him and his props.** **Must read warm-affectionate-boss, NEVER
gangster/menacing/triad-caricature** (the load-bearing dignity item — 老大 carries a boss/gang-leader
connotation, so the warmth is what makes this safe; **consultant sign-off + blind-test required before final art**).
