# Vendor art — 29 distinct per-vendor illustration briefs (V7-M)

> **Every brief below opens with the canonical style line** and must be read against
> [`art/art-style.md`](art-style.md) (the one look). These are briefs for a **human illustrator** — per the
> studio rails there is **NO AI final art**; AI is only ever a labelled, gitignored *mockup* to explore a
> brief. A **paid Taiwanese cultural + language consultant** reviews all vendor art before final/print. Real
> places, dishes, and romanisation follow [`design/vendor-places.md`](../design/vendor-places.md).

**The style line to open every brief:**
> *Style: per `art/art-style.md` — fun, lighthearted, **stylised** cartoon (Agent Avenue-style cartoon look;
> Taiwan street-food travel-agent theme); clean confident linework, bold flat colour with simple stylised shading,
> night-market palette (warm day golds/ambers → neon night violets/teals); at-a-glance readability at card
> size. Human-illustrated; no AI final art.*

## Binding rails (apply to every vendor)
- **Secular rail (BINDING):** frame the **market / street / dish / stall** only — **never** temple, deity,
  ghost-month or funerary imagery, even where a place sits near a temple (N04 near 松山慈祐宮, N03 near 善導寺;
  **N10 割包 in 艋舺 sits by 龍山寺 — frame the shopfront/street only, NEVER the temple**;
  大稻埕 deliberately anchored to Dihua St + the Wharf, not the 慈聖宮 food street).
- **Real dishes never mangled; 繁體字 throughout;** each vendor is a **distinct** dish + stall + place +
  composition — no two alike (the deck should read as a tour across the island's real food scene).
- **Named-stall spot-checks (consultant, before final art — stalls can close/move; treat as illustrative):**
  阜杭豆漿 (N03), 富盛號 (S02), 鄭老牌木瓜牛奶 (S03), 太空紅茶冰 (C06, confirm still lists 冬瓜茶), 世祖胡椒餅 (N04),
  阿璋/北門口肉圓 (C01), 度小月 (S06), 沙卡里巴 coffin-bread shop (S05, succession/closure press — place + 1942
  invention are solid). **Dish ≠ place-signature** flagged for N01 (Houlong), C02 (二市場), E02 (羅東): frame as
  *place-anchor + a generic-but-real dish*, not as "the place's specialty".
- **Indigenous items → the #1/#2 pre-art gate:** **E04 石板烤肉 (Paiwan 排灣族 / Rukai 魯凱族) is the #1 pre-art
  consultant item** and **E01 竹筒飯** the second — confirm the specific community + framing; **no generic
  "tribal" iconography**; the consultant supplies any Indigenous-language terms (**do NOT ship AI
  romanisation** for E01/E04).
- **Romanisation to confirm (consultant):** S04 火雞肉飯 Tâi-lô; all Indigenous terms (E01/E04); and the
  new day dishes **N09 飯糰, C07 大麵羹, E06 蔥油餅** (Tâi-lô to consultant).

## Day vs Night — the two visual roles (BINDING · new V7-M)
The reshaped deck gives day and night vendors **different jobs**, and the art must make the two read apart at
a glance:
- **DAY food vendors = ENGINE SEEDS.** Cheap, plentiful, *everyday* street-food — the humble morning grab,
  the on-the-house bite you pick up on the way through. On the card face they carry the **region discount
  icon** (the little ▲北/◆中/●南/■東 emblem). Illustrate them **warm, humble, generous, everyday** — warm
  daytime golds/ambers, cheap-and-cheerful energy, a friendly small-stall feel. These are engine pieces, not
  prizes; do **not** give them trophy grandeur. (N01, N02, N09, N10 · C01, C02, C07 · S01, S02, S07 · E01,
  E02, E06.)
- **NIGHT food vendors = TROPHIES.** Scarce, expensive **marquee prizes**. They now carry **NO region icon**
  — the card face reads as a *prize, not an engine piece*. Render them with **trophy weight**: the hero dish
  given the boldest, most dramatic, most appetising presence in the deck, deep **neon night palette**
  (violets/teals), a sense of occasion. Apex trophies get the single heaviest presence of their region. The
  no-icon night trophies are **N04, N05, N06★ · C04, C05★ · S04, S05, S06★ · E03, E04★ (top apex)**.
- **Drinks are the cheap connective tissue** — never engine seeds, never trophies; they carry no region icon
  and lean whichever palette their setting suggests (a poised day teahouse reads warm; a wharf night cup
  reads neon). Day drinks: N03, C03, C06, S03, E05. Night drinks (cheap clock-fillers): N07, N08.
