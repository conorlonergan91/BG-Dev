# Mock-up kickoffs — paste one at a time (AFTER uploading the art-context zip)

**Workflow (phone-friendly):**
1. Upload **every `.md` from `ta-art-context.zip`** to ChatGPT as context/knowledge, in one go.
2. Then paste **one kickoff block below per generation.** They're short on purpose — the full art
   direction (the look, night-market palette, the "convey the warmth" goal, the SCALE TABLE, and the
   binding dignity + secular rails) lives in the uploaded `art-style.md` and the per-asset briefs, so
   ChatGPT already has it. The kickoff just points at the right asset + the make-or-break constraints.

Mock-ups only — final art is human-illustrated (no AI final art). After generating, drop the PNG into
`games/travel-agents/build/mockups/` (or send it to me) named by its FIG code, and I'll transparent-key,
fit, and embed it into the board render automatically.

Each kickoff already asks for the clean cut-out (single subject, portrait, flat pale background, NO dark
vignette/glow, high-res PNG) so the image drops straight into the component.

**Best results (CD's finding): ATTACH a liked earlier character as a STYLE REFERENCE image** when you paste
the prompt, and tell the tool to match that art style — it locks the look far better than words alone and
keeps the cast cohesive. Build the set outward from your favourite one.
