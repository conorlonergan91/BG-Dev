# Character mock-up prompts — ready to paste (playtest props, NOT final art)

**Phone workflow:** each block below is a **complete** prompt — the style/warmth anchor is already
baked in, so you just **copy one block → paste into ChatGPT/DALL·E → generate**. No assembling. One
character per generation (don't ask for the whole cast at once — it comes back as a grid you then have
to slice).

**Ask for a clean cut-out** (these words are in each prompt): *single character, portrait orientation,
plain flat pale background, NO dark vignette/glow, high-res PNG.* That flat background is what lets me
key it transparent cleanly — the dark-glow versions (the first Councillor & Granny) can't be cut out.

**After you generate:** these are **MOCK-UPS** only (final art is human-illustrated). Drop the PNGs into
`games/travel-agents/build/mockups/` (or send them to me here) and I'll split/transparent/fit them and
drop them into the board render automatically.

A warm, lighthearted, over-the-top-but-dignified stylised cartoon character illustration — the fun, characterful look of "Agent Avenue" but travel-agent / Taiwanese street-food themed (no spies). GOAL: radiate WARMTH — the friendly, food-loving spirit of Taiwan's night markets, felt at a glance. One foot up on a little red electric mobility-scooter, leaning into the go. Behind her, a faint cue to her go-anywhere reach — four little region lights or a high-speed-rail line. Age is her ADVANTAGE, never her frailty: upright, energetic, twinkling, winning the race. Do NOT draw her hunched, cane-dependent, confused, or as a butt of the joke.
