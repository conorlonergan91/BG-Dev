## 0. The pitch (read aloud, 20 seconds)
"You're rival **travel agents** building the best Taiwan food **tour**. Ride the rails, source real
ingredients from the regions that actually grow them, and plate the island's great dishes into your
tour. Race your rival to lock the **Hot-List** spots before they do, chase your own **secret signature
itinerary**, and roll with an island that won't sit still — a festival opens a window, a typhoon shuts a
road. Best tour is named **Top Agent**."

## 1. Goal
Score the most points — from **plated Dishes**, the public **Hot-List** spots you lock in, small gains
from the island's **Conditions** and your head-office **Briefs**, and your **secret signature itinerary**
(+7, revealed at the end). Most points = **Top Agent**.

## 2. Setup (teach while you set up)
1. Unfold the **Taiwan map** — four regions in a ring: **North 北 · Central 中 · South 南 · East 東**
   (the HSR line links North–Central–South down the west coast). The board also holds the **Season
   clock**, the **score track**, the **Hot-List row (3 spots)**, and the **Condition queue (2 slots)**.
   Put the **Season** marker and each agent's **score marker** at 0.
2. Stock the ingredient supply: **each region's specialties** by that region, plus the **pantry** pile
   (the convenience store — available anywhere).
3. Shuffle the **Dish deck**; deal a **Menu** of **4** face-up (5 at 3p, 6 at 4p).
4. Shuffle the two **Operation** decks separately. Deal **3** face-up from the **Hot-List deck** into the
   Hot-List row.
5. Seed the **Condition queue**: draw 1 to **ACTIVE**, 1 to **NEXT** (both face-up).
6. Each agent: take a **pawn** (start on **opposite** regions at 2p — one North, one South); **draw 2
   Sealed Operations, keep 1 secret** (your **signature itinerary**), return the other face-down; take a
   small opening hand of **3 Briefs**. Your Larder starts empty and stays **face-up in the open** — your
   signature itinerary is the only thing you keep hidden.

## 3. How a turn works (the core loop — teach these four, then just play)
Your **Larder** sits **face-up** in front of you all game — no hidden hand; the *only* secret is your
signature itinerary. On your turn, do **ONE** action. (You may also **play one Brief**, free, and — when
a Plate completes a Hot-List spot — **lock it in**, free.) Then if your Larder is over **7**, discard
down to 7.

- **STOCK UP** — take **2 ingredients** where you stand (this region's specialties and/or pantry).
- **TRAVEL** — move to a **neighbouring** region, then take **1 ingredient** there. (North↔South is a
  neighbour by HSR.)
- **PLATE** — pick a **Menu** Dish you can pay for, spend its recipe from your Larder, add the Dish to
  your tour (**move your score marker up now**). Refill the Menu. **Advance the Season +1.** Then **lock
  in** any Hot-List spot your tour now completes (see §5).
- **EXTRACT** — file your finished tour / catch the last train home: declare your trip done and trigger
  the end-game (§7).

> **BRIEFS** (head-office memos — play one per turn, free): a **safe boost** ("Free Sample": take 1
> extra ingredient here · treat 1 recipe ingredient as any type when plating · travel 1 extra region), a
> **risky gamble** ("All-In": discard 2 ingredients now, then your next Plate scores +2), or a **light
> nudge** ("Sold Out!": name a region — your rival can't take its specialty on their next turn). Warm,
> never mean — one-turn delay, never destroy.

## 4. Sourcing & recipes (the "aha": travel because the food lives there)
- You can only take a region's **specialty** while you're **in** that region — oysters in the South, tea
  in the Central hills, rice & mountain greens in the East, beef/pork/chicken/egg in the North.
- **Pantry** supplies are at the convenience store in **every** region.
- A **Dish** is a little recipe of 2–3 named ingredients. To plate the Chishang bento you need East rice
  *and* Northern pork — so you travel. *(That's the whole game in one sentence.)*

## 5. The Hot-List (racing your rival)
Three public **Hot-List** spots sit face-up — bonus tours worth **4–6 VP** (e.g., "a Southern seafood
crawl," "the round-island classic"). Both agents are racing for the same spots. The **moment your tour
satisfies a spot's condition** (usually right after you Plate), **lock it in**: take the card, **score
its VP now**, advance the **Season +1**, and refill the row from the deck. First to complete-and-lock
takes it — your rival is shut out of *that* one, but a fresh spot slides in.
- The same plated Dishes count toward the Hot-List **and** your secret signature itinerary — one trip,
  many payoffs.
- Because everything's face-up (the spots, both tours, the score track), a snipe is a race you saw
  coming — outraced, not robbed. Your Dishes still scored, and still chase the next spot.

## 6. The Season & Conditions (the island doesn't wait for you)
Every **Plate** and every **lock-in** nudges the **Season** marker **+1**. When it crosses a
**milestone**, the island turns: the **ACTIVE Condition** ends, **NEXT** slides into ACTIVE, and a new
Condition is drawn to NEXT (you always see one coming). Conditions open **windows** (a festival makes a
region's food worth more / easier to stock) or throw **hazards** (a typhoon taxes travel into a region;
a landslide shuts a pass) or bring **warm characters** (a one-off boon for everyone). The **final
milestone** can end the game (§7).

*(Milestones at 4/8/12/16 for 2p, final ~20–24; scale up at higher counts — see design-doc. ~4–5
Conditions fire per game.)*

## 7. Ending the trip
The end-game begins the moment **either**: a player takes **EXTRACT** (catches the last train), **or**
the **Season** hits its **final milestone**. Then: **finish the current round**, play **one more full
round**, and stop.

## 8. Scoring (reveal signature itineraries now!)
Your score marker already shows your **open** points — plated Dishes, locked Hot-List spots, and any
Condition/Brief gains. Now **flip your signature itinerary**: **+7** if you pulled it off, else 0. Add it
on. Most points wins → **Top Agent**. **Ties:** most Hot-List spots, then most Dishes, then most regions
visited.

## 9. The warm-tone reminders (margin asides, not rules)
- You're a travel agent *promoting* Taiwan's food to clients — a fan sharing the good stuff, never
  extracting it.
- Conditions are the island living its life — festivals, weather, kind locals — never sacred customs
  played for laughs; local characters are warm, real people, never a punchline.
- Dish names stay real (Tâi-lô + 繁體). The comedy is the mission gag and your own gluttony — never the
  food or the place.

## Teaching sequence (FTUE — the order a first game should reveal rules)
1. **Goal + one Dish** — "here's beef noodle; it needs beef (North) + a pantry staple. Let's get it."
2. **Stock Up / Travel** — source the two ingredients; feel the "travel because the food's there" turn.
3. **Plate** — score it (marker moves!), nudge the Season; point at the Condition queue ("this is live
   now; that one's coming").
4. **Hot-List** — "these three public spots are worth bonus points and your rival wants them too — the
   instant your tour completes one, lock it in." (The teachable moment for the direct race.)
5. **Signature itinerary** — "here's your secret headline tour; +7 if you nail it — keep it face-down."
6. **Briefs** — introduce the opening hand once someone hits a natural moment (a full Larder → the
   risky-gamble Brief; a rival about to snipe → the nudge).
7. **Extract / end-game** — introduce once the Season is ~half full.
