# Travel Agents: Taiwan — Rules (v6.3.1 · TICKETS-ONLY · balance-validated)

<!-- Voice/provenance/terms: design-doc.md §"Rules-draft provenance (relocated)". Canon: v6.3-content-draft.md. -->

## 0. The pitch (read aloud)
"Rival **travel agents** build the best Taiwan food **tour** in one eating day. Ride between four regions to
spread your name, dig ingredients, and pile **Buzz** on the vendors you want — but **your hype can't outrun
your Reach.** Win a vendor by out-hyping your rival on the spot; each becomes a stop and makes the next
cheaper. Points come only from **Tour Tickets** — finish them before **dusk** turns the street to the night
market, and again before the last train. Most tour points is **Top Agent**."

## 1. Goal
**Points come only from your Tour Tickets** — your win-pile **成團** minus half your lose-pile **遺珠** (§9).
Most = **Top Agent**. Vendors don't score — they're your engine and your tour stops (§5).

## 2. Setup (teach while you set up)
1. Unfold the **Taiwan map**: four regions in a ring (**North 北 · Central 中 · South 南 · East 東**), an HSR
   spine linking North↔South. On it — the **day-clock** (a **dusk** and a **last-train** mark), the **Tours
   Completed** counter (**Rave Review** marks at your **2nd** and **4th** completed tour), the **Reach**
   track (your Buzz-cap, *not* a score), and per region a face-down **vendor deck** with **face-up vendor
   slots** (North 2, others 1) and a face-down **ingredient market deck**. Set each Tours Completed to 0 and
   each Reach to **2**.
2. Split each region's cards into a **DAY** and a **NIGHT** deck (drinks are low-Draw vendors mixed into
   each); set the night decks aside for dusk. Shuffle the day vendor and day ingredient decks into place.
   Ingredient decks hold the 5-palette (**N 豬肉 · C 茶 · S 海鮮 · E 米**, plus common **K 糖** in **C & S**
   only) and a few **events** (§6) — including the **two 排班保養 "Scheduled Maintenance"** cards, one in the
   **North NIGHT** and one in the **South NIGHT** ingredient deck. Fill the vendor slots. Set out the
   **Buzz** supply (~24 @2p).
3. **Tour Tickets:** shuffle the DAY deck, **draw 5** (rest to the box), reveal **3** as the shared **ticket
   row** (the other 2 form its draw pile); set NIGHT aside, drawn likewise at dusk. A ticket's deck is its
   clock: **DAY judged at dusk, NIGHT at the last train** (§8–§9).
4. Each agent takes an **Agent Character** (ability + flaw, read aloud) and a **pawn**, and **starts in
   Taipei (the North hub).** If **里長伯** is in play, he names **one home region** now — his −1 discount
   applies only there, +1 ingredient to Bribe elsewhere (§5). Your Larder starts empty, **face-up** all
   game.

## 3. Your turn (teach these six, then just play)
Take **ONE** action. Then if your Larder exceeds **7**, discard down to 7 (to that region's market discard,
reshuffled when the deck runs dry — ingredients are never removed from the game).

- **VISIT THE LOCAL MARKET** *(where you stand)* — flip the top **3** of this region's ingredient deck; any
  **event** fires now by scope (§6), then discards. **Keep up to 2.** *Greedy Grab:* keep **1 more** → **−1
  Reach** (§4a).
- **CONVENIENCE STORE** *(anywhere)* — flip the top **1** of **any** region's ingredient deck: ingredient →
  take it; event → fires **by scope** (§6). *Greedy Grab:* take the **next card too** → **−1 Reach** (§4a).
- **TRAVEL** — move to a **neighbour** (HSR = North↔South), then flip the top **1** of the region you
  **arrive** in: ingredient → take it; **event → nothing, keep moving**. **+1 Reach** (§4).
- **PROMOTE** *(with a vendor, in this region)* — place **2 Buzz** from the supply on vendor(s) you're with,
  keeping your **total committed Buzz ≤ your Reach** (§4). Buzz stays on the card, public; rivals may pile
  on too.
- **BRIBE A VENDOR** — win a face-up vendor by out-hyping your rival, on the spot (§4–§5).
- **LINE UP A TOUR TICKET** — take one from the face-up **row of 3** (this phase's pool) as an open
  commitment, then refill. Only one you **haven't yet completed**. It scores later into your win/lose pile
  (§8–§9).

> **Agent Characters bend the rules.** Each mat has **one ability + one flaw** for that agent alone — read
> them aloud at setup; a mat wins where it differs from the above.

## 4. Buzz & Reach — the hype fight
- **Buzz** sits only **in the supply** or **on a vendor** — never in your hand.
- To Buzz a vendor, **stand in its region**.
- **Both agents can Buzz the same vendor.**

> **Your hype can't outrun your Reach.** Your **total committed Buzz may never exceed your current Reach**
> (a marker starting at **2**; not a score). **Travel +1** and a few **events** raise it; **winning a vendor
> clears your Buzz off it**, freeing the cap. To out-escalate a contested vendor you first need the Reach to
> cover the stack.

### 4a. Reach sinks — the Greedy Grab
You may **grab one extra ingredient beyond your limit** (an extra keep on a **Visit**, or a second card on a
**Convenience Store**) — each grab costs **−1 Reach**. A couple of **events** also cost Reach (a bad review,
a mix-up).

> **The one edge-case rule (say it once):** your committed Buzz can never exceed your Reach. So a **Greedy
> Grab you can't afford is simply not allowed** (take it only if it wouldn't drop your Reach below your
> committed Buzz); and if an **event** drops your Reach below your committed Buzz, **immediately return Buzz
> from the board to the supply — your choice which — until you're back within your cap.** (Reach never goes
> below 0.)

## 5. Bribing a vendor — one rule for all
To **Bribe** a face-up vendor, all at once:
1. be **with the vendor, in its region**;
2. hold **at least 2 more Buzz on it than your rival** (the majority, checked right now);
3. **pay its ingredients**, minus your region discounts.

> **里長伯's home turf (his mat only).** His **−1 discount works only in his home region**; Bribing
> **anywhere else** costs **+1 ingredient** of any one type. His mat carries both icons.

Take the vendor to your tour — now a **stop** and an **engine piece**. If it completes a Tour Ticket you
hold, flip that ticket to its **done** side and **advance your Tours Completed counter**. Then **clear ALL
Buzz on it** (yours *and* your rival's) to the supply — **freeing your Reach cap** — **refill** the slot,
and **move the day-clock +1** (§7). The vendor is locked from your rival.

A vendor card carries a **Draw** number (人氣) and **either a region icon or none** — no perks, and **Draw is
not a score.** Draw only feeds the few Tour Tickets that ask for it (e.g. *"vendors totalling ≥12 Draw"*)
and marks the big prizes. **Each region icon you hold makes that region's ingredients 1 cheaper** on future
vendors — the engine. **Drinks and the apex ★ carry no icon; Buzz is never discounted. Night stalls cost
meaningfully more than day stalls, and every night name needs two regions' ingredients** — so a one-region
ramp helps you *afford* the big night names but never zeroes them. If your rival out-hypes you first, your
Buzz fizzles back to the supply, you keep every ingredient you sourced, and you never seize a place.

> **Rave Reviews (milestones · 好評).** The **first time YOU reach your 2nd completed tour → +1 Reach; the
> first time YOU reach your 4th → take 1 ingredient of your choice + your next Bribe costs 1 less
> ingredient.** One-time each, and **each agent earns their own** (crossing a mark never pumps the leader).

## 6. Market decks & events
Each region's market is a **face-down deck** of its **signature** ingredient (+ the common **K**, in two of
the four regions) and a few **event** cards (mostly hazards). **Working a market — a Visit or a Convenience
Store — fires any flipped event straight away, by its scope; a card flipped as you *arrive* on a Travel does
nothing.**
- **Hazards** *(the majority)* **tax or delay — never destroy** what you hold, and never cost a whole turn.
- **Boons** *(the minority)* hand out Buzz, or make a vendor **get hot** (★).

> **The scope tag — who the event hits**, read from the region the card came out of: **⦿ You** (the flipper)
> · **⬢ This region** (everyone standing there) · **➤ Next door** (a neighbour). On a **Visit** you stand
> where you flipped, so ⬢ hits **you**; on a **Convenience Store** you're often elsewhere, so ⬢ hits
> **whoever stands in the region you flipped** — so a scoped hazard can land on a rival. Never a personal
> attack — just the island living its life.

> **排班保養 "Scheduled Maintenance" (a table-wide event).** An island-wide card (its own banner frame,
> *not* a ⦿/⬢/➤ card): **the HSR is down for everyone.** Place it on the HSR spine. Until the **start of
> your next turn**, North and South are **not** linked — everyone Travels the long way (N→C→S or N→E→S).
> Then discard it and the HSR reopens.

> **★ Star vendors.** Some night-market names carry a **printed ★** (a real Michelin / Bib-Gourmand nod),
> the top **Draw**, and **no region icon** (a pure trophy) — one apex per region. A boon can also drop a
> **★ Star token** on a vendor, making it worth **+2 Draw** to whoever wins it.

## 7. The day-clock — Day → Night
It's **one eating day**. The day-clock climbs **+1 every time anyone Bribes a vendor**. Two marks (2p:
**dusk = the 5th table-wide vendor win · last train = the 10th**; the marks grow with more players):
- **Act I — Daytime 白天:** morning food + drinks; build your discount ramp. **Daytime Tour Tickets** are on
  offer.
- **DUSK (the day-clock hits the dusk mark).** In one flip: **judge every claimed DAYTIME ticket**
  (completed → win-pile · unfinished → lose-pile — §9); then **clear the entire day layer island-wide** —
  every face-up **and** face-down day vendor deck **and** day ingredient deck — and reveal each region's
  **night decks**. Swap the ticket row to the **nighttime** pool. **You keep your Larder.** Any unclaimed
  day vendor is gone.
- **Act II — Night Market 夜市:** the big stalls — expensive, fought-over, the ★ apex per region.

## 8. The last train home
When the day-clock hits the **last-train mark** (2p: the **10th vendor win**), everyone catches the last
train: **finish the current round, play one more full round, and stop.** *(Backstop: if a region's deck
empties, its slot isn't refilled until the next flip; if all decks dry island-wide, the phase ends then. If
one win trips **dusk and** the last train at once, resolve **dusk first**, then the end check.)*

## 9. Scoring
**Your score is your Tour Tickets, and nothing else.** Reveal both piles and tally once, at game end:
- **+ the printed value** of each ticket in your **WIN pile (成團)** — the tours that ran.
- **− floor(value ÷ 2)** for each ticket in your **LOSE pile (遺珠)** — the ones that got away.

Tickets are sorted into your two piles as each phase ends (DAY at dusk, NIGHT at the last train — §7–§8);
you tally **once**. **Vendors do NOT score** — each was your engine and a stop; its Draw was never points.
**Reach does NOT score.** Most points wins → **Top Agent**. **Ties: most vendors, then most regions covered,
then fewest tickets in your LOSE pile (遺珠).**

## 10. Warm-tone reminders (not rules)
- You're **promoting** Taiwan's food — winning a vendor is out-hyping your rival to earn a vendor's blessing,
  never taking from them. "Bribe" is the cheeky word; the deed is a warm hard sell, not corruption.
- Events are the island living its life — never sacred customs for laughs; a Convenience-Store event on your
  rival is the *island* doing it, a shared groan, never a spiteful strike. Dish and vendor names stay real
  (Tâi-lô + 繁體); the comedy is the caper gag and your own gluttony.
