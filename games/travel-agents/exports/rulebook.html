Travel Agents: Taiwan — Rules

Two rival travel agents race to build the island's best Taiwan food tour in one eating day. Ride between four regions, dig up ingredients, and pile Buzz on the vendors you want. Win a vendor by out-hyping your rival on the spot — each becomes a stop on your tour and makes the next one cheaper. Your only points are Tour Tickets: finish the most before the last train and you're Top Agent.

  • ① The map — four regions in a ring: North 北 · Central 中 · South 南 · East 東, with an HSR line linking North↔South. Each region gets a face-down vendor deck and a face-down ingredient deck. Split every deck into a DAY and a NIGHT pile; shuffle the DAY piles into place and set the NIGHT piles aside for dusk. (Drinks are just cheap vendors mixed through both.)
  • ② Vendor slots — fill the face-up slots: 2 in the North, 1 elsewhere.
  • Each ingredient deck holds its signature — N 豬肉 · C 茶 · S 海鮮 · E 米, plus common K 糖 in Central & South only — and a few events. Seed the two 排班保養 Scheduled Maintenance cards into the North and South NIGHT ingredient decks (§6).
  • ③ Tracks — every Reach marker to 2, every Tours Completed counter to 0.
  • ④ Buzz supply — a shared pool (about 24 for two players).
  • Tour Tickets — shuffle the DAY deck, draw 5 (box the rest), reveal 3 as the shared row (the other 2 are its draw pile). Set the NIGHT deck aside for dusk.
  • Each agent takes an Agent Character (read its ability + flaw aloud), a pawn in Taipei, and an empty Larder kept face-up all game.

3. Your turn

Take ONE action. Then, if your Larder holds more than 7 ingredients, discard down to 7.

  • VISIT THE LOCAL MARKET (where you stand) — flip the top 3 of this region's ingredient deck. Any event fires now (§6), then discards. Keep up to 2 ingredients.
  • CONVENIENCE STORE (anywhere) — flip the top 1 of any region's ingredient deck: take the ingredient, or the event fires (§6).
  • TRAVEL — move to a neighbour (HSR = North↔South), then flip the top 1 of the region you arrive in (event? nothing happens, keep moving). Gain +1 Reach.
  • PROMOTE (with a vendor here) — place 2 Buzz from the supply onto vendor(s) you're standing with (§4). Buzz stays on the card, in full view — your rival can pile on too.
  • BRIBE A VENDOR — win a face-up vendor you're standing with by out-hyping your rival (§5).
  • LINE UP A TOUR TICKET — take one from the row of 3 (then refill). You may only take one you haven't already completed. It scores later (§8).

Reach also gets spent: a Greedy Grab costs -1, and a couple of events (a bad review, a mix-up) ding it too. It never drops below 0. (If Reach would fall below your committed Buzz, see the FAQ.)

5. Bribing a vendor

To Bribe a face-up vendor, all at once: 1. be with it, in its region; 2. hold at least 2 more Buzz on it than your rival (checked right now); 3. pay its ingredients, minus your region discounts.

Take the vendor onto your tour. If it completes a Tour Ticket you hold, flip that ticket to its done side and slide your Tours Completed counter up one (§7). Then clear ALL Buzz on the vendor — yours and your rival's — to the supply, refill the slot, and move the day-clock +1 (§7). The vendor is now locked to you.

What a vendor is. A vendor shows a Draw number (人氣) and either a region icon or none. Draw isn't a score — it just feeds the tickets that ask for it and marks the big prizes. Each region icon you hold makes that region's ingredients 1 cheaper on future vendors — that's your engine. Drinks and the ★ apex have no icon, and Buzz is never discounted. Night stalls cost more than day ones and each needs two regions' ingredients, so one region's ramp helps you afford them but never makes them free. Out-hyped first? Your Buzz just fizzles back to the supply — you keep everything you dug up and lose only the stop.

7. Day → Night

It's one eating day, and the day-clock climbs +1 every time anyone Bribes a vendor. It has two marks (for two players: dusk = the 5th win · last train = the 10th; both grow with more players).

  • Act I · Daytime 白天 — morning food and drinks; build your discount ramp. Daytime tickets are on offer.
  • DUSK (the clock hits the dusk mark) — all at once: judge every claimed daytime ticket (done → win pile · unfinished → lose pile), clear the whole day layer island-wide, reveal each region's night decks, and swap the ticket row to the nighttime pool. Keep your Larder; unclaimed day vendors are gone.
  • Act II · Night Market 夜市 — the big stalls: pricier, fought-over, the ★ apex.
  • LAST TRAIN (the clock hits the last-train mark) — everyone heads home: finish the current round, play one more full round, and stop.

8. Scoring

Reveal both piles and tally once: - + the printed value of each ticket in your WIN pile — COMPLETED (成團) — the tours that ran. - - floor(value ÷ 2) for each ticket in your LOSE pile — CANCELLED (取消) — the tours that didn't run in time.

Vendors, Draw, and Reach score nothing. Most points is Top Agent. Ties: most vendors, then most regions covered, then fewest tickets in your lose pile.

FAQ

  • My Reach would drop below the Buzz I already committed. A Greedy Grab that would do this simply isn't allowed. If an event forces it, return Buzz from the board to the supply — your choice which — until your committed Buzz is back within your Reach. Reach never goes below 0.
  • An ingredient deck runs out. Reshuffle its discard — ingredients never leave the game.
  • A vendor slot's deck is empty. Leave the slot unfilled until the next flip refills it. If every deck dries up island-wide, that phase ends immediately.
  • One Bribe trips dusk and the last train at once. Resolve dusk first, then check the end.
  • Do drinks or big-Draw vendors score? No. Only Tour Tickets score — vendors are pure engine.
