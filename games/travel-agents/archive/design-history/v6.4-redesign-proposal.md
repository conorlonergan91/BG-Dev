## CHANGE 1 — BOOKINGS + a hand limit

### The rule
- **Bookings** are the renamed Tour Tickets. Claiming one is **"booking a tour."** Mechanically identical
  to the v6.3.1 Claim action (take one from the face-up row of 3; it refills; judged into your
  Complete / Cancelled pile at its phase close).
- **Hand limit:** you may hold at most **N in-progress bookings** at once.
- The board reserves **N card-scale "booking slots"** along its top edge (per player, in the player's
  play area — or a shared strip with owned zones at 2p; bg-infodesign's call).

### RECOMMENDATION on N — **N = 3** (A/B/C below)

**Grounding from the last sim:** the winner *held* **2.38 bookings at end** (`tickets_held_winner
2.381`), and a booking leaves your hand the moment its pattern is met (it slides to the Complete pile —
see below). So the realistic *working set* a player juggles is **2–3**: usually one near-complete + one
or two speculative. A cap must sit at or just above that working set so it bites the **hoarder** without
taxing normal play.

- **(A) N = 3 — RECOMMENDED.** Sits exactly one above the ~2.4 working set. A normal player never feels
  it; a hoarder who fans out claims to deny the row or blanket-cover patterns is capped. Matches the
  face-up row size (3) as a legible mental model ("hold as many as the row shows"). Lowest disruption to
  the validated intransitive band.
- **(B) N = 2.** Bites often — forces hard completion focus, kills speculative claims. Would meaningfully
  reshape play (and interacts badly with the −½ Cancelled penalty, which exists precisely to *discipline*
  speculative claims — N=2 pre-empts that decision instead of pricing it). Only choose if the FTUE shows
  players drowning in open bookings. Higher balance risk; **needs re-sim.**
- **(C) N = 4.** Barely binds (winner holds 2.4) — near-cosmetic anti-hoarding, mostly a physical-slot
  convention. Choose if the CD wants the slots purely as table furniture, not a live constraint.

### At the cap and want a new booking — **RECOMMENDED: cancel-to-claim**
> **You may hold at most 3 in-progress bookings. If all 3 slots are full you cannot Claim — UNLESS, as
> part of the Claim, you first CANCEL one held booking (move it to your Cancelled pile, taking its
> −floor(v/2) at scoring). Completing a booking always frees its slot immediately (it leaves your hand
> for the Complete pile).**

- This keeps full agency (you *can* always pivot to a hotter booking) but **prices** it — swapping a
  speculative booking costs its cancellation penalty, which is thematically perfect ("cancel a tour to
  book a better one") and leans on the already-locked −½ penalty rather than adding a new rule.
- Alternative (rejected as default): *forbidden* (can't Claim at cap, full stop). Cleaner to state but
  can strand a player behind a dead booking they can't shed except by waiting for phase-close; the
  cancel-to-claim escape is warmer and more agentive. Offer it as the fallback if the penalty double-hit
  feels punishing at the table.

### Do Day/Night bookings share the limit? — **YES, one shared set of 3 slots**
DAY bookings are judged at **dusk**; a DAY booking cannot complete after dusk, so **at dusk every
in-progress DAY booking is swept to Cancelled**, emptying all slots for Act II. So during Act I you hold
DAY bookings, during Act II you hold NIGHT bookings, and the **same 3 physical slots** serve both — no
separate limit needed. (Edge, for the teach: a DAY booking whose pattern you meet only *after* dusk still
counts toward the Tours-Completed track per the v6.3.1 rule, but it has already gone to Cancelled for
scoring — harmless, unchanged from canon.)

### "Completing frees the slot" — the timing rule this depends on
Under tickets-only, a booking's pattern is met by vendors you've *won and keep* (monotonic, irreversible).
The moment the pattern is met, the booking flips to its **done** face, **ticks the Tours-Completed track
+1**, and **leaves your hand for your Complete-pile display** — freeing its slot immediately. (Its *score*
still settles at its phase close, unchanged.) So the hand limit governs **in-progress** bookings only,
exactly as the CD framed it.

### Balance impact vs the last sim
**Low.** Bots held ~2.4 and rarely wanted more, so N=3 changes near-nothing for the validated lines —
the intransitive band (five playstyles ~7pt; five characters 43.5–55.2%) should hold. The **one lane to
watch** is a breadth/collection line that fans out many cheap bookings at once (`n_collect6`,
`g_collect5`, food-count ladders) — N=3 could pinch it if a human wants 4+ open collection bookings. In
sim this lane isn't dominant, so the risk is low, but **a light re-sim confirm is cheap** (see §Re-sim
matrix). **Not make-or-break.**

## CHANGE 2 — FOLLOWERS (rename of Buzz+Reach) and the CAP MODEL — **THE KEY FORK**

### What's fixed regardless of the fork
- **Followers** = the tokens (renamed Buzz). They live in a supply. **Promote** places **2** Followers
  (網紅: 3 — see Change 4) from supply onto a co-located vendor.
- **You do not "gain" Followers into a hand** — they are always available in supply; the constraint is
  the cap. **What you build is the CAP** (your Followers-max = how many Followers you may have committed
  on the board at once). This is the old Reach, renamed.
- **Start:** committed Followers = 0; **Followers-max starts at 2** (you can run one Promote from turn 1).
- **Winning a vendor** (Bribe) **clears your Followers off it back to supply** → frees that much of your
  cap for the next campaign. Unchanged.
- **The cap invariant:** your total committed Followers may never exceed your Followers-max. Unchanged
  from the v6.3.1 rule (§1a/1d canon), including the forced-return edge case.

**The CD's ask:** delete the Reach *track* and show the cap as a **printed max number in a square** on
the player board. The fork is whether that number can still **grow**.

### THE FORK — A/B (with the sim evidence that decides it)

**The sim evidence the fork lives or dies on** (`sim-results.md`, v6.3.1, N=1200):
- Followers-max **grows from 2 → ~7.5 over a game** (`reach_start 2.000` → `reach_end_mean 7.459`;
  `reach_at_dusk_mean 3.861`; `reach_mean_all 4.496`).
- Peak *committed* Followers is only **~3.7** (`max_committed 3.722`; `max_buzz_on_vendor 3.480`).
- **The cap actively bites ~2×/game** (`reach_blocked_per_game 2.015`) — i.e. the growth is *load-bearing*
  early, when max is 2–4 and a player wants to escalate a fight but can't yet.
- Growth is fuelled overwhelmingly by **Travel** (`reach_gain_travel 13.059`/game ≈ 6.5/player), topped
  up by events (`reach_gain_event 2.592`) and milestones (`reach_gain_ms 1.431`), and **burned** by the
  greed sinks (`reach_loss_grab 3.349`, `reach_loss_event 2.815`).

Read together: **the ceiling rarely matters (nobody commits 7); the RAMP from 2 is the whole point.** The
validated design win is *"the agent who travelled more can out-escalate a collision, and Travel — the old
dud action — becomes the engine's fuel line."* A fixed cap deletes that ramp.

- **(A) Fixed per-character Followers-max (a printed number, no growth).**
  - *What it costs:* **retires the entire validated push-pull.** Travel reverts to the weakest action (it
    no longer buys anything — the sim explicitly rescued Travel *by* making it Followers-max income).
    **The greed sinks lose all meaning** (−1 Followers-max on a Greedy Grab is inert if the max can't
    move; `reach_loss_grab 3.349` of validated push-pull evaporates). **The catch-up valve dies** (a
    behind player could travel to out-escalate; fixed removes it).
  - *What you'd have to REPLACE (all un-simmed):* (1) a new Travel reward (e.g. Travel arrives with +1
    ingredient, or draws 2) so it isn't a dead action; (2) a new cost for the Greedy Grab (e.g. return a
    committed Follower, or −1 ingredient next turn); (3) a new early-game escalation tension (a fixed cap
    of ~5–6 means you can commit big from turn 1 → the fight loses its build). That's **three un-simmed
    replacements cascading off one change** — a full economy re-validation.
  - *If chosen anyway:* fixed Followers-max ≈ **5** for the field (covers the ~3.7 peak commit + the
    大哥 ≥1-lead and 網紅 promote-3 headroom), a higher number for 網紅 (Change 4). But I do not recommend
    it.
  - *Purity win:* it is a genuinely printed static number — the cleanest "max in a square."

- **(B) Growing cap, track-free — RECOMMENDED.** Keep the ceiling rising (Travel +1, ~3–4 events +1,
  minus the greed sinks) exactly as validated, but represent it **on the square itself** with a tiny
  moving element instead of a board-length track:
  - a **small rotary dial** set into the square (a wheel showing 2–12 through a window), **or** a
    **peg/slider notch** around the square's edge, **or** a stack of small **+1 chits** placed in the
    square.
  - *What it preserves:* **everything the last sim validated** — the ramp, Travel-as-fuel, the greed
    sinks, the catch-up valve, the "out-travel to out-escalate" signature depth. **Zero balance delta.**
  - *What it costs:* one small per-player component and a sliver of "printed number" purity — the number
    changes, it isn't static. That is the whole price.

**RECOMMENDATION: (B).** The CD's stated goal — kill the long board *track* and put the cap *in a square*
on the player board — is **fully achieved by B** (a dial/slider IS a number in a square). B honours the
CD's ergonomic intent while keeping the validated economy; A honours a stricter reading of "printed
static number" but pays for it by retiring a sim-validated subsystem and spawning three un-simmed
replacements. If the CD's true priority is the *static-number aesthetic* over the economy, that's a
legitimate call — but it is a **from-scratch economy redesign**, not a cosmetic one, and I'd want that
stated explicitly before we spend the re-sim on it.

### How the followers questions resolve under each model
| Question | (A) Fixed | (B) Growing — RECOMMENDED |
|---|---|---|
| How you gain Followers | always in supply; not gained | always in supply; not gained |
| Start committed | 0 | 0 |
| Followers-max start | printed static ≈ 5 (網紅 higher) | 2 (all), grows to ~12 |
| How the cap moves | never | Travel +1; ~3–4 events +1; greed sinks −1 |
| Promote cost/effect | place 2 (網紅 3) ≤ max | place 2 (網紅 3) ≤ max |
| Winning a vendor | Followers return to supply, free the cap | same |

**⚠ BALANCE DELTA + RE-SIM FLAG.** Model **B is a pure rename → cosmetic, no re-sim needed** for the cap
economy itself (same numbers, new component). Model **A is a major economy change → a gated re-sim is
REQUIRED** and must re-validate the three replacement mechanics before this is trusted. Either way,
nothing here ships to the FTUE until the chosen model is re-confirmed.

## CHANGE 3 — RAVE REVIEWS → a "Mountain Goats" end-game VP race

### The two moves
1. **Relocate the Tours-Completed counter OFF player boards and BACK onto the MAP** as a **single global
   track**, with each player's completion marker on it (reverses last pass's board relocation; restores
   the Mountain-Goats-style shared race the players can all read).
2. **Swap the reward** from the current `perplayer` **fuel boons** (2nd tour → +1 Reach; 4th tour →
   ingredient + next-Bribe −1) to **end-game VP tokens** grabbed in **arrival order, descending value**:
   the **first player to exceed N completed bookings takes the highest Rave-Review token; the next to
   reach it takes the next; …** Tokens score at game end.

### RECOMMENDATION — threshold, token count, and the VP ladder

**RECOMMENDED: single race, threshold N = 2 (i.e. the moment you complete your 3rd booking), one token
per player in arrival order, SHALLOW descending values.**

**Why N = 2 (3rd completion):** from the last sim, the 2nd-completion mark fired for **~1.44 players/game**
(`ms7_earn_players 1.443`) and the 4th-completion mark for **~1.15** (`ms14_earn_players 1.145`). So:
- A **high** threshold (exceed 3 / 4th completion) is reached by **~one player only** → not a race, a solo
  bonus handed to the leader → **maximally snowbally.**
- A **low** threshold (exceed 2 / 3rd completion) is reached by **~1.4 players** → the leader always, the
  trailer often → a genuine "who got there first" race where the trailer still frequently collects the
  small token. **Least snowbally, most race-like.**

**Token ladder (shallow — the primary snowball guard):**
| Count | Tokens (arrival order, descending) |
|---|---|
| 2p | **{2, 1}** |
| 3p | {3, 2, 1} |
| 4p | {3, 2, 1, 0} (or {4,3,2,1} if the CD wants everyone rewarded) |

- **Single threshold, one token each** (not a token at every completion-mark). Each player grabs exactly
  one token — their arrival-order token — so no one can compound multiple grabs. This is the least-runaway
  form of the Mountain-Goats mechanic.
- **Keep the top token small (≤ ~⅓ of the expected margin ~6–7).** {2,1} means the completion-leader nets
  **+1 over the trailer** — a nudge with teeth, not an amplifier.

**Multiple rungs vs single race:** I recommend a **single race** (fewer tokens, lighter, one grab each).
Multiple rungs (a token at completions 2, 3, 4…) reward whoever's fastest at *every* rung = pure
leader-pump; avoid.

### Reconcile with tickets-only scoring
**Confirmed: the core score is UNCHANGED** — Σ(Complete-pile booking value) − Σ(floor(v/2) Cancelled-pile
booking). **Rave-Review tokens are a purely ADDITIVE end-game bonus** layered on top. Tickets remain the
dominant vector; the tokens are a small speed/breadth bonus.

### ⚠ Snowball / double-dip analysis (the bg-critic concern — this is the load-bearing risk)

**The concern is real and this change directly reverses a sim-validated fix.** The `perplayer` milestone
model was adopted *specifically because* the old "first-to-cross-for-the-table" model let one player take
both marks in **67–80%** of games and compound a runaway; `perplayer` fixed it (band unchanged, "zero
runaway"). A global descending-VP race is structurally a **leader-pump** again — so the default assumption
must be that it re-opens that door until re-sim says otherwise.

**Is it a double-dip?** Partially, and the *partial* is the saving grace:
- The core score rewards completion **VALUE**; the Rave-Review race rewards completion **COUNT**. These
  are **semi-orthogonal**. A player who completes **4 cheap bookings** (value ~3 each) *wins the count
  race and grabs the big token* while *trailing on core value*; a player who completes **3 fat bookings**
  (value ~6 each) leads core value but loses the count race. So the token frequently lands on the
  **breadth player who's behind on value** — a mild **catch-up**, not an amplifier.
- The **true double-dip only fires when the same player leads BOTH count and value** (the genuine runaway
  leader). The **shallow ladder ({2,1}, +1 net) caps how much that case can amplify** — it cannot, on its
  own, turn a close game into a runaway.
- **Mitigating structural fact:** the game-end trigger is the **vendor-claims clock (10th claim @2p)**,
  **not** completions — so racing completions does *not* also let the leader slam the game shut. The
  endgame-driver playstyle benefits, but doesn't get to end the game early on top.

**Proposed mitigations (recommend adopting 1+2):**
1. **Shallow ladder** ({2,1} @2p) — the primary guard (above).
2. **Low threshold** (exceed 2) so the trailer usually also collects a token — turns a solo bonus into a
   consolation-bearing race.
3. *(Reserve, only if re-sim shows runaway)* score the token on **breadth the core score under-rewards** —
   e.g. cap it so completing *cheap* bookings is the efficient way to win the race, making it an explicit
   depth-vs-breadth fork rather than a value amplifier.

### The hidden cost the CD should price in: this REMOVES the validated fuel boons
Swapping Rave Reviews from fuel→VP **deletes** the +1 Reach (Mark 1) and ingredient+discount (Mark 2)
boons. Those were doing real work in the validated economy:
- **`reach_gain_ms 1.431`/game of Followers-max income disappears** (~10% of non-Travel Reach income). Minor
  if Change 2 = B (Travel still supplies 13/game), but **it also removes the Mark-1 catch-up valve** (a
  behind player got +1 max on their 2nd completion).
- **⚠ If Change 2 = A (fixed cap) AND Change 3 removes the boons, the ENTIRE Reach/push-pull layer is
  gutted** (no Travel-fuel, no milestone-fuel, no sinks, static cap) — the game goes static. This is a
  strong additional argument for Change 2 = B.

### A/B/C for Change 3
- **(A) Global Mountain-Goats race, N=2, shallow ladder {2,1}, fuel boons removed — the CD's ask, best
  form. RECOMMENDED as the version to sign off IF the CD wants this change** — but explicitly **gated on
  a re-sim** for runaway, and note it dents the "one score vector" elegance headline (tickets-only becomes
  tickets + a minor bonus vector).
- **(B) Status quo: keep `perplayer` fuel boons, no VP race** (reject the change). The safe, validated,
  elegance-clean option. The fallback if re-sim shows runaway.
- **(C) HYBRID — RECOMMENDED as the risk-managed middle.** Move the counter to a **global map track** (the
  visible race the CD wants) **and** keep **Mark 1's +1 Followers-max fuel boon** (`perplayer` —
  preserving the validated Reach income + catch-up valve), while adding **one shallow end-game token for
  the count-leader** ({1} or {2} @2p top-only, or {2,1}). This gives the CD the visible global race and an
  end-game VP hook **without** deleting the validated economy — the smallest change that satisfies the
  intent. Slightly heavier (keeps a boon AND adds a token), so bg-elegance weighs in.

**My overall steer:** if the CD's priority is *the visible race + a VP payoff*, do **(C)**; if the
priority is *a clean Mountain-Goats VP mechanic and they accept re-validating the economy*, do **(A)** with
mitigations 1+2. Either **requires the gated re-sim** (runaway + the fuel-boon economy delta).

## CHANGE 4 — INFLUENCER (網紅) rework

**Current validated kit** (`characters.csv`, re-sim **51.2%**, dead-centre in band): *Going Viral —*
*Promote places 3 Buzz (+1 over standard), still capped by Reach; Flaw: goes last (no lost turn).*

The CD proposes either **(A)** gains N followers at the start of each phase, or **(B)** a higher
Followers-max than the others — and notes this interacts with the Change-2 cap model.

### The honest read first
Under the **Followers** rename, the influencer's *existing* validated ability — **"your Promote places 3
Followers"** — already resonates perfectly ("your posts reach more people per post") and sits at **51.2%,
exactly in band, with sim evidence behind it.** Neither of the CD's alternatives has sim support, and both
*compound* (more cap / more free followers → win more fights → the influencer is the character the sim
already watches for heat). So the **lowest-risk, most-resonant move is to KEEP Promote-3** and simply
reframe it in Followers language. I'd put that on the table as the primary.

### If the CD wants the cap to BE the influencer's identity — pick (B), tuned to the Change-2 model
Between the CD's two options, **(B) higher Followers-max** is the more thematic and cleaner-interacting:
"the influencer has more **reach**" is the literal fiction now that Followers are the resource, and it
plugs straight into whichever cap model Change 2 lands on. **(A) gains-N-per-phase** is a lumpy tempo
burst that's the hardest to tune and the least clean — I'd avoid it.

**Numbers for (B), by Change-2 model — and DROP Promote-3 if you adopt it (do not stack both, or 網紅
goes hot):**
- **If Change 2 = A (fixed cap):** field Followers-max = 5; **網紅 = 7 (+2)**; Promote reverts to 2. The
  influencer's whole edge is the bigger static reach — the single cleanest kit, and it's exactly what a
  fixed-cap model makes elegant.
- **If Change 2 = B (growing cap):** **網紅 starts Followers-max at 4 (+2 head start)** (or ceiling +2),
  Promote reverts to 2. The early head-start lets the influencer escalate fights a couple of turns before
  rivals — thematic, and it decays in relative terms as everyone's cap grows (self-tapering, which helps
  keep them in band).

**Flaw:** keep **"goes last"** as the counterweight under any option (it was the validated flaw at 51.2%).

### RECOMMENDATION
- **Primary: KEEP "Promote places 3 Followers"** (reframed as *Going Viral*), flaw *goes last*. It is
  already validated at 51.2%, it resonates cleanly with the rename, and it needs **no re-sim**. The rework
  is arguably unnecessary.
- **If the CD insists on their A/B: choose (B) higher Followers-max**, tuned to the Change-2 model as
  above, **dropping Promote-3**, and **re-sim** — a higher/earlier cap compounds against the fight and
  must be re-banded (target ±10pt, no crusher; the field was 43.5–55.2%). **Avoid (A).**

**Interaction note:** (B) only has a clean home if Change 2 keeps the cap meaningful. Under Change 2 = A
it's the natural influencer kit; under Change 2 = B it's a head-start. Under *no* cap at all (a degenerate
Change-2-A-plus-Change-3 outcome where the Reach layer is gutted), a Followers-max kit would be inert —
another reason to land Change 2 on B.

## Knock-on changes summary

### Rulebook terminology (for bg-elegance / bg-theme)
- Global find/replace: Buzz → **Followers**; Reputation/Reach → **Followers-max**; Tour Ticket/mission →
  **Booking** (claim = "book a tour"); WIN pile → **Complete pile** (COMPLETED 成團); LOSE pile →

- **New concepts to glossary-check:** the **hand limit** (Change 1) and **Rave Review VP tokens** +

  concept — confirm ≤15 terms and re-check the ≤1850-word rulebook after the rewrite.
- **Elegance flag:** Change 3 converts the "one score vector" (tickets-only) headline into "tickets + a
  minor end-game bonus vector." Defensible if the tokens read as a simple speed bonus, but it must be
  called out in the budget re-check — it is the single biggest elegance cost in v6.4.

### Tokens / components
- **Followers tokens** = the renamed Buzz tokens (~24 @2p). **No new component — a rename.**
- **Retire the Reach track marker.** Under **Change 2 = B**, replace with a **small per-player dial /
  slider / chit-stack** in the Followers-max square (new small component). Under **Change 2 = A**, no
  replacement (static printed number).
- **NEW: Rave-Review VP tokens** — a small set (2 @2p, up to 4 @4p; e.g. values {2,1}/{3,2,1}). New
  component (Change 3).
- **Global Tours-Completed track on the map** — relocate the counter from player boards back to a single
  shared map track, with each player's existing completion marker on it (Change 3). No new marker, just
  relocated + made shared.
- **Booking slots** — N=3 card-scale reserved zones (top edge / player area). Board-print, not a piece.
