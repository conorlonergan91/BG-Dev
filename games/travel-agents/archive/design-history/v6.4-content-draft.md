## Change 1 — THE PROMOTE / FOLLOWERS MODEL, stated unambiguously (resolves the CD's incoherence)

> **The CD spotted a real incoherence in the old "網紅: Promote places 3" wording** once the cap became a
> dial: *"Doesn't Promote always place N followers based on the slider's current position? So 'places 3'
> doesn't make sense."* The resolution below states the model plainly and reconciles it exactly with the
> validated sim.

### 1a. What Followers-max (the dial) IS — a CEILING, not a per-Promote amount

**Followers-max is the maximum number of Followers you may have COMMITTED on the board at once — a
ceiling on your total, across all vendors.** It is *not* the amount you place per action, and Promote
does *not* "fill you up to the dial." The dial's value only ever says *how much total committed Followers
you are allowed to have out right now.*

### 1b. What ONE Promote does — LOCKED

> **PROMOTE** (you must be in the region, with the vendor(s)): **place 2 Followers from your supply onto
> one or more co-located vendors** — in any split you like (2 on one vendor, or 1 + 1 on two) — **provided
> your TOTAL committed Followers, across every vendor, never exceeds your Followers-max (the dial's current
> value).** If placing 2 would break the ceiling, you place only as many as fit (and if 0 fit, you cannot
> Promote this turn).

- **Promote places a FIXED 2 Followers per action** — the validated value, **unchanged from v6.3.1**
  (網紅 no longer places 3 — see §Change 4). The **dial caps your running total**, not the per-Promote
  amount.
- **Winning a vendor (Bribe) clears your Followers off it back to supply → frees that much of your ceiling**
  for the next campaign. Unchanged.
- **Followers-max never limits placement to "one big dump"** — because each Promote only adds 2, building
  a deep commitment on a contested vendor takes **several Promotes across several turns**, which is
  precisely why you must travel to raise the ceiling *and* spend the actions to fill it. Two separate
  costs (a ceiling you raise by Travelling; the Promote actions that fill it), and that is the fight's
  whole tempo.

### 1c. Reconciliation with the validated sim (why this is the same game, not a change)

The last validated sim (`sim-results.md`, v6.3.1, N=1200) shows **`promotes_per_game 11.805`** (~5.9
Promotes/player) but a **peak total committed of only `max_committed 3.722`**, with the ceiling ramping
**`reach_start 2.000` → `reach_end_mean 7.459`** and biting **`reach_blocked_per_game 2.015`** times/game.
These numbers are **only consistent with "Promote places a fixed small batch (2), capped by a rising
ceiling"** — you place in 2s, a win clears some, and you rarely stack past ~4 at once even though the
ceiling climbs toward ~7.5. A "Promote fills up to your dial" model would have shown committed ≈ the dial
(~7.5), which the sim does NOT. **So the fixed-2 model IS the validated economy** — stating it explicitly
is a clarification, not a mechanical change, and needs **no re-sim.**

*(Why not "Promote places 1"? The CD floated it as an example. Place-1 would halve per-action commitment
and change the Promote/tempo economy the sim validated at place-2 → it would force a re-sim for no
benefit. **Locked at place-2.**)*

## Change 2 — Followers-max as a GROWING DIAL (rename + component; economy identical)

**The cap is unchanged in every value and behaviour — only its name and its physical representation change.**

- **Rename:** the live cap is now **Followers-max**. Buzz tokens are now **Followers**.
- **Representation:** **DELETE the standalone Reputation/Reach board track.** Show Followers-max as a
  **small dial (a rotating wheel showing 2–12 through a window) or a slider notch set into a printed
  square on each player board.** The dial IS "a number in a square" — it just moves.
- **The economy is carried forward from v6.3.1 UNCHANGED** (read `v6.3-content-draft.md §Change 1` for
  full detail — every value below is identical, only the noun changed):
  - **Start:** committed Followers = 0; **Followers-max starts at 2** (one Promote of 2 from turn 1).
  - **Grows by:** **Travel → +1 Followers-max** (Travel is the engine's fuel line — the validated rescue
    of the dud action; `reach_gain_travel 13.059`/game); **~3–4 events → +1** (`reach_gain_event 2.592`).
  - **Burned by (the greed sinks — INTACT):** the **Greedy Grab** (keep +1 extra ingredient on a Visit, or
    take a 2nd on a Convenience Store → **−1 Followers-max**; `reach_loss_grab 3.349`/game), and ~2–3
    Rep-cost events (負評 / 出包 → **−1 Followers-max**; `reach_loss_event 2.815`/game).
  - **The dial floors at 0, tops at 12**, and **the forced-return edge case is unchanged** (a forced loss
    that would drop Followers-max below your committed Followers makes you return Followers from the board,
    your choice which, until committed ≤ Followers-max; a voluntary Greedy Grab you can't afford is simply
    illegal). See `v6.3-content-draft.md §1d` — this rule is verbatim, "Reputation" → "Followers-max".
- **Balance: ZERO delta** — same numbers, same push-pull, same catch-up valve. **Cosmetic; no re-sim.**

## Change 3 — Missions/Tickets → BOOKINGS, with a 3-slot hand-limit

### 3a. Rename
- **Bookings** = the renamed Tour Tickets (the sole scoring vector). Claiming one is **"booking a tour."**
  All Booking patterns, values, decks, and judging triggers are the **44-card expanded set carried forward
  UNCHANGED** from v6.3.1 §4b–4c (DAY 20 / NIGHT 25; ×1.0 values; DAY judged at dusk, NIGHT at the last
  train). Only the word changes.

### 3b. The hand-limit — LOCKED N = 3
> **You may hold at most 3 IN-PROGRESS Bookings at once.** The board reserves **3 card-scale Booking slots
> along its top edge** (per player play-area; bg-infodesign places them). A Booking occupies a slot only
> while in progress.

- **Completing a Booking frees its slot immediately.** The instant a held Booking's pattern is met by
  vendors you've won and keep, it flips to its **done** face, **ticks your Tours-Completed counter +1**,
  and **leaves your hand for your Complete-pile display** — vacating the slot. (Its *score* still settles
  at its phase close; the counter tick and slot-free are immediate.)
- **At the cap and want a new Booking — CANCEL-TO-CLAIM:**
  > **If all 3 slots are full you cannot Claim — unless, as part of the Claim, you first CANCEL one held
  > Booking** (move it to your Cancelled pile, taking its **−floor(value ÷ 2)** at scoring). You may always
  > pivot to a hotter Booking, but it costs the cancelled one's penalty.
- **Day/Night share the 3 slots.** At **dusk**, every in-progress DAY Booking is swept to Cancelled (a DAY
  Booking cannot complete after dusk), emptying all slots for Act II. So you hold DAY Bookings in Act I and
  NIGHT Bookings in Act II through the **same 3 slots** — no separate limit.
  - *(Carried edge, unchanged: a DAY Booking whose pattern you meet only after dusk still ticks the
    Tours-Completed counter, though it has already gone to Cancelled for scoring — harmless.)*

### 3c. Grounding + balance read
The last sim's winner **held ~2.38 Bookings** (`tickets_held_winner 2.381`), so **N=3 sits one above the
normal working set** — invisible to healthy play, biting only the hoarder. Expected balance delta: **low**;
the intransitive band (five playstyles ~7pt; five characters 43.5–55.2%) should hold. **One lane to
confirm in the re-sim:** a breadth/collection line (`n_collect6`, `g_collect5`, food-count ladders) that
might want 4+ open Bookings — low risk (not dominant in sim), but a **light re-sim confirm** is cheap.

## Change 4 — Rave Reviews KEPT (renamed), Tours-Completed stays per-player, and the Influencer's higher Followers-max

### 4a. Rave Reviews — the validated per-player boons, kept (rename only)
**No Mountain-Goats VP race, no map race-track.** The `perplayer` milestone boons that the v6.3.1 re-sim
validated (they removed the ~67–80% runaway) are **kept exactly**, renamed to the new vocab:

- **Tours-Completed counter stays PER-PLAYER on each character board** (the CD reverses the proposed map
  relocation — it never moves off the board).
- **Marks at your 2nd and 4th completed tour** (`perplayer` — each agent earns their own; cannot pump the
  leader):
  - **Mark 1 (2nd completed tour) → +1 Followers-max** (was "+1 Reach"; raises your dial — a fuel/catch-up
    boon, no points; a *gain* never triggers the forced-return edge case).
  - **Mark 2 (4th completed tour) → take 1 ingredient of your choice, and your next Bribe costs 1 fewer
    ingredient.** (Unchanged.)
- **Balance: ZERO delta** — identical boons, identical eligibility, identical `reach_gain_ms 1.431`/game.
  **Cosmetic; no re-sim.**

### 4b. Scoring — TICKETS-ONLY, carried forward UNCHANGED
> **Your final score is your Booking tally, and nothing else:**
> **Σ(value of each Complete-pile 成團 Booking) − Σ(floor(value ÷ 2) for each Cancelled-pile 取消 Booking).**

Vendors do NOT score; Followers-max does NOT score. Rave Reviews are non-VP fuel boons (no tokens, no VP).
Tiebreak: **most vendors → most regions covered → fewest Cancelled-pile Bookings.** Expected 2p winner
score ~10–14 (sim ~12.8; margin ~6–7). All verbatim from v6.3.1 §Scoring, vocabulary swapped.

### 4c. Influencer (網紅) — DROP "Promote places 3", give a HIGHER Followers-max — LOCKED value + re-sim flag

**Why the change:** under the clarified model (§Change 1), Promote places a fixed 2 for everyone and the
dial is a *ceiling*. "網紅 places 3" muddied that (it read as if the per-Promote amount were the point).
The clean, resonant influencer identity now that **Followers are literally the resource** is *"the
influencer has more reach"* — expressed directly as a **higher Followers-max.**

> **網紅 — Going Viral (LOCKED): your Followers-max starts at 4 instead of 2** (you may have up to 4
> Followers committed from turn 1). It then rises exactly like everyone's (Travel +1, events +1, greed
> sinks −1), so **you carry a persistent +2 ceiling all game.** **Promote places 2, the same as everyone.**
> **Flaw (kept): goes last** (last seat in turn order; not a lost turn).

**Why "higher START" and not "higher CEILING":** the sim shows the ceiling itself is almost never the
binding constraint (peak committed only `max_committed 3.722`; end-mean ceiling `7.459`) — **the cap bites
EARLY**, when Followers-max is 2–4 (`reach_blocked_per_game 2.015`, mostly early). So a higher ceiling
(e.g. dial to 14) would be nearly inert; a **higher START (4)** puts the influencer's edge exactly where
the cap actually bites — she can escalate and hold contested vendors a couple of turns before rivals can,
and the edge self-tapers in *felt* terms as everyone's dial climbs past the ~3.7 peak-commit zone. This is
a front-loaded, self-tapering identity — good design.

- **Concrete value: start Followers-max = 4 (baseline 2 → +2). Same ramp, same 12 ceiling. Promote = 2.
  Flaw = goes last.**
- **⚠ THIS NEEDS A RE-SIM.** Previously 網紅 was *Promote-3 + goes-last = 51.2%* (dead-centre). Swapping to
  *start-max-4 + goes-last* is an untested trade whose value the sim must measure. **The re-sim must
  confirm the five-character band stays tight** (last sim 43.5–55.2%: 網紅 51.2 · 大哥 50.7 · 大胃王 54.8 ·
  里長伯 55.2 · 媽寶 43.5), that 網紅 lands **≥45% and ≤~55% with no crusher**, and that the +2 head-start
  doesn't distort the contested-fight rate (`contested_frac`) or over-fuel the Reach economy.
  **Reserve dials (only if the re-sim reads it out of band):** start **3** (+1) if hot; start **4 with a
  faster ramp** (Travel gives 網紅 no extra, but drop the goes-last flaw) or **+3** if cold. Start-4 with
  goes-last is the opening bid.

## Change 5 — Base TRAVEL sources NO ingredient; the Homebody is the sole Travel-sourcer

> **The CD spotted that Travel wrongly implies an ingredient gain.** v6.4 fixes it: **base Travel is
> movement + dial-fuel only.** This also resolves a genuine contradiction in the carried canon (see §5c).

### 5a. The rule — LOCKED
> **TRAVEL (base):** move to a neighbouring region (HSR links N↔S) and **gain +1 Followers-max.** **That
> is all.** There is **no arrival flip and no ingredient drawn or kept.** (Events are moot on a base Travel
> — there is no card flip; Scheduled Maintenance continues to fire **only** on a Visit / Convenience-Store
> flip.)

- **Travel is NOT a dead action** — its validated primary job survives intact: it is the **Followers-max
  fuel line** (+1 to the dial, `reach_gain_travel 13.059`/game in the last sim) and the map-mover. It
  simply stops doubling as an ingredient source. This keeps the two gates cleanly separate: **Travel fuels
  the Followers-max gate; Visit / Convenience Store fuel the ingredient gate.**

### 5b. The EXCEPTION — the Homebody (媽寶, C2) is the sole Travel-sourcer
> **媽寶 — Mum's Packed Lunch (UNCHANGED kit, now genuinely unique): on a Travel, draw the top card of the
> region you LEFT and keep it** (an ingredient). No other character sources on a Travel.

- **This makes 媽寶's ability distinctive for the first time.** Under the carried sim it drew the *left*
  region while everyone *else* drew the *arriving* region — a directional edge only. Now that base Travel
  sources nothing, 媽寶 is **the only agent who sources while travelling** — a clear, thematic "swears the
  food back home tastes better" identity.
- **Event on 媽寶's Travel-draw → WHIFFS** (LOCKED). Consistent with Travel arrivals never firing events:
  if 媽寶's left-region draw surfaces an event (incl. Scheduled Maintenance), it does **not** fire — return
  it to the deck (Maintenance keeps waiting for a Visit/CStore). Only the ingredient case is kept. *(This
  matches the current sim behaviour, so the sub-decision adds no extra balance delta.)*

### 5c. Contradiction cleaned up (for the implementation pass)
The carried canon is self-contradictory on base Travel and must all resolve to §5a:
- `rules-draft.md §Actions` (~L46): "flip the top 1 of the region you arrive in" — **DELETE.**
- `rules-draft.md §Events` (~L104): "a card flipped as you arrive on a Travel does nothing" — **DELETE**
  (there is no arrival flip anymore).
- `v6.3-content-draft.md §Turn` (~L721): "flip the arriving region's top card (event whiffs), and gain +1
  Reputation" — **superseded** by §5a (move + +1 Followers-max, no flip).

### 5d. Balance impact + the sim finding (why this is a CORE re-sim item — CASE 1)
**Checked the sim's actual behaviour** (`sim/travel_agents.py` `_do_travel`, L1502–1527; `_bump_src`
L1424): **base Travel currently SOURCES an ingredient** — it draws 1 card from the *destination* region
and, if it's an ingredient, keeps it (adds to the larder, increments `src_by_region`); events whiff.
媽寶 (`office`) draws the *left* region instead. So this is **CASE 1: removing base-Travel sourcing is a
real balance change (less total sourcing).**

- With `travels_per_game 13.371`, base Travel is a **material fraction of total sourcing today** — removing
  it meaningfully lowers ingredients-per-game for four of the five characters and for the field.
- **⚠ Metric caveat for the re-sim:** `_bump_src` currently counts **every Travel as a sourcing action**
  in the CD-4 night-sourcing metric (`src_*_per_turn`), whether or not an ingredient is kept. When base
  Travel stops sourcing, `_bump_src` **must be updated** so a base Travel is **not** counted as a sourcing
  action (only 媽寶's Travel-draw + Visit + CStore count) — otherwise the night-sourcing rate is inflated
  and the CD-4 re-check is meaningless.

### 5e. The two knock-ons — both go into the re-sim scope
1. **Night sourcing / CD-4.** The soft night curve was validated on *"night sourcing = 113% of the day
   rate"* counting Visit + CStore + **Travel**. With Travel no longer sourcing, the re-sim must confirm
   night sourcing still clears the **~60–100%-of-day** target. **Do NOT change the night curve on paper** —
   flag it as a re-sim watch; if night sourcing drops below the target, the lever is the curve (or a small
   clock nudge), decided *after* the sim, not now.
2. **Homebody win rate.** 媽寶 was the **~43% floor** (the sim's known greedy-bot blind spot on its routing
   ability). As the **sole Travel-sourcer**, its relative sourcing edge grows — a **probable organic
   floor-fix**. The re-sim must confirm it lifts 媽寶 toward band **without overshooting the top** (watch it
   doesn't blow past ~55%). If it overshoots, the reserve lever is to draw-but-choose-to-keep, or cap the
   frequency — decided after the sim.

## Core loop (v6.4 vocabulary — a turn = ONE action)

Do **ONE** action, then discard down to Larder cap **7** (recycle rule unchanged):

- **VISIT THE LOCAL MARKET** *(where you stand)* — flip 3 of this region's ingredient deck, events fire by
  scope, **keep up to 2** (媽寶/大胃王 per kit). **Greedy Grab:** keep +1 more → **−1 Followers-max** (only
  if it wouldn't break your committed-Followers ceiling).
- **CONVENIENCE STORE** *(anywhere)* — flip the top 1 of any region's deck; ingredient → take it, event →
  fires by scope. **Greedy Grab:** take the next card too → **−1 Followers-max** (same ceiling check).
- **TRAVEL** — move to a neighbour (HSR = N↔S) **and gain +1 Followers-max** (raise your dial). **Base
  Travel sources NO ingredient — no arrival flip, nothing kept; its whole job is to fuel the dial + move.**
  (Only the Homebody 媽寶 sources on a Travel — see §Change 5.)
- **PROMOTE** *(with the vendor, in this region)* — **place 2 Followers from supply onto co-located
  vendor(s), in any split, provided your total committed Followers stays ≤ your Followers-max (the dial).**
  (See §Change 1 — a fixed 2 per action, capped by the dial's running total.)
- **BRIBE A VENDOR** — out-hype your rival on the spot (≥2 Follower majority; 大哥 ≥1) + pay ingredients
  (− region discounts) → win it: it joins your tour as an **engine piece** (region-icon feeds the discount
  ramp; may **complete a held Booking** → advance your per-player Tours-Completed counter, freeing a
  Booking slot), its Followers clear to supply (freeing your ceiling), refill the slot, move the
  vendor-claims clock +1, lock the rival out. **No points** — vendors don't score.
- **BOOK A TOUR (Claim a Booking)** — take one from the face-up row of 3 (current phase's DAY or NIGHT
  pool); it refills. **You may hold at most 3 in-progress Bookings — at the cap, first Cancel one to make
  room** (§Change 3). Judged into your Complete 成團 / Cancelled 取消 pile at its deck's phase close — **the
  sole scoring vector.**

Every turn presents a meaningful decision (`forced_turn_frac 0.000`, `avg_legal_actions 9.606` in the last
sim); no dead/forced moves are introduced.
