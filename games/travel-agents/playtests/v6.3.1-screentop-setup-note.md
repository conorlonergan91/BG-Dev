# v6.3.1 — Screentop setup note (stand up the 2p FTUE room)

> Written by `bg-prototyper` for the first **v6.3.1** human FTUE. **TICKETS-ONLY scoring · soft
> night-cost curve · common Taipei start.** Everything here is **regenerated from data** by `./make.sh`;
> the play-ready upload set is **`games/travel-agents/exports/screentop/`** (one `*_sheet_1.png` + one
> `*_back.png` per pile). The board, tracks, mats, token key, cover, rulebook and player aid live at the
> top of **`exports/`**. Theme: **night-market**. Card cells are 409×585 px; every deck sheet is within
> Screentop's ≤10×7, ≤4096 px limits (verified — largest is `ticket_night` at 3681×1755).

## The one-screen mental model (what scores, what doesn't)
- **Your ONLY score is Tour Tickets.** Completed → **COMPLETED 成團 WIN pile** (+value); unfinished at its
  phase's judging → **CANCELLED 取消 LOSE pile** (−floor(value/2)). Tally once at game end.
- **Vendors give NO points.** A vendor = a **Draw (人氣) attribute + a region icon or none**. Vendors are
  the *engine* (region-icon discount ramp + the stops that complete tickets). Their Draw is only read by
  the few value-referencing tickets and marks the ★ apex.
- **Two non-score tracks + one clock:** **Reach** (your Buzz cap, start 2), **Tours Completed** (a
  completions meter that hosts per-player Rave Reviews at your 2nd & 4th tour), and the **vendor-claims
  day-clock** (dusk @5, last train @10 at 2p).

## The day/night read
Cards are recoloured by `phase`: **DAY = warm gold frame + ☀ badge + "日 DAY" ribbon**; **NIGHT =
neon-violet frame + ☾ badge + "夜 NIGHT" ribbon**. Region colour carries *where* (North vermilion△ ·
Central teal◆ · South amber● · East indigo■); phase carries *when*. All badges are drawn SVG (no tofu).

## How to import a deck in Screentop
New Game → add a **Deck** → upload the `*_sheet_1.png` as the face, set **Columns × Rows** to the grid in
the tables below, and set the deck **Back** to the matching `*_back.png`. The **last cell of every face
sheet is a concentric-ring back** (harmless — the standalone back overrides it). A few decks have **one
trailing blank cell** (noted below) — delete that single blank card after import, then **shuffle** the pile.

## 6. Stand-up sequence (fast)
1. Board in **day** state; deal the 4 DAY market decks + 4 DAY vendor decks; flip face-up rows (**North 2,
   others 1**). Hold all NIGHT decks aside.
2. Both pawns in **Taipei**. Reach markers to **2**, Tours-Completed to **0**, day-clock at START.
3. Deal character mats; **里長伯 picks a home region openly**.
4. Shuffle **DAY tickets**, draw 5, reveal a row of 3. Hold NIGHT tickets aside.
5. Set out Buzz / ★ / ingredient supply and each player's 成團 / 取消 zones.
6. Play Act I. **At the 5th Bribe = DUSK:** judge DAY tickets into WIN/LOSE piles, clear the day market +
   vendor layer island-wide, flip the board to **night**, deal the 4 NIGHT market + 4 NIGHT vendor decks,
   draw 5 NIGHT tickets → row of 3. Play Act II to the **10th Bribe = LAST TRAIN**; judge NIGHT tickets;
   tally 成團 − ½·取消.
