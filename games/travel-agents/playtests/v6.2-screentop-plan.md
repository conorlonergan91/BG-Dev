## 2. The feel questions — what the sim can't judge (the reason for the session)
Ranked by how load-bearing the unknown is:

1. **The Buzz fight (RISK #1, the make-or-break).** The sim can't seat two bots onto the same vendor
   (~5% contested by construction). At a table: **do players actually collide and pile Buzz on the same
   vendor?** When they do, is the ≥2-majority tug-of-war **tense and fun**, or a slow slog / a feel-bad
   ("I outbid you, your Buzz fizzles")? Does spreading vendors across 4 region markets make collisions
   feel **too rare** (everyone courts a different region) — the v6.2 worry? *Instrument:* count contested
   courts/game; note every moment two players wanted the same vendor and how it resolved; ask each player
   post-game "did the fight feel like the heart of the game, or a side event?"
2. **The disruptor's take-that warmth (RISK — the weakest lane, 46.7% in sim).** When a player fires a
   **scoped Convenience-Store event onto a rival's region**, does it land as **cheeky "bad luck, the
   island happened to you"** or as a **mean strike**? Is leaning disruptive a **fun lane** or does it feel
   toothless (the sim says viable-but-marginal)? *Instrument:* count deliberate rival-facing CS flips; after
   each, ask the *target* how it felt (warm vs spiteful — the binding rail); ask the disruptor if the lane
   felt worth committing to. If toothless → the fix is *more contest-reward* (a higher-value contest
   ticket / a small Rep or Buzz kickback for winning a contested vendor), **not** nastier events.
3. **The mission press-your-luck + the −½ penalty (the elegance A/B the sim couldn't settle).** Bots only
   claim finishable tickets, so the sim saw **no behavioural difference** with/without the penalty. The
   human question: **do players claim speculatively** (grab a juicy ticket they're not sure they'll
   finish)? **Does claiming feel like a gamble** — the TtR "book it and race the penalty" tension? *This is
   the decider for the penalty fork.* *Instrument:* tickets claimed vs completed vs abandoned; note every
   claim the player deliberated over; ask "did you ever grab a ticket as a gamble?" If nobody agonises →
   the penalty is inert and `bg-elegance` can cut it; if they do → keep it.
4. **The dusk flip — the mood turnover.** Does clearing both day layers and swapping to the neon night
   deck **land as a felt beat** ("the street changes in front of you"), or a fiddly reset? Any **dead
   reset turn**? Is the **event-heavier night** hectic-**fun** or swingy-**punishing**? Should the **night
   half be a shorter sprint**? *Instrument:* note the table's reaction at dusk; count any dead/admin turns
   around the flip; ask whether the night felt hotter and whether it overstayed.
5. **Teachability / FTUE / component load.** Time-to-teach; can two new players parse **5 face-up vendors
   across 4 regions + a 3-ticket row + 2 face-down piles/region + Buzz-on-vendors** at a glance? What did
   they misread? (This is `bg-elegance`/`bg-infodesign`'s component-load flag, tested live.) *Instrument:*
   teach-time, first-turn confusions, any rule looked up twice, the "what does this icon mean?" moments.
6. **Pacing (the sim ran long).** Bots took ~25 turns/player (~30–40 min); the design targets ~25–30 min /
   14–18 turns. *Do humans play tighter?* *Instrument:* total time, turns/player, whether it dragged —
   especially the late game and whether the chosen clock's dusk landed at the right moment.
7. **Central under-pull (the new travel flag).** The sim shows Central under-visited (13% sourcing) because
   tea has no export demand. *Do players visit Central?* If it feels like a dead region, confirm the data
   tweak (give tea one export in the cross-region cycle).
8. **網紅's new lost-turn flaw — a KEY balance-check (CD refinement, NOT sim-equivalent).** The CD replaced
   網紅's vague "starts behind" with a concrete **"misses its first turn of the night phase"** (one lost
   turn). **The catch:** the sim seated 網紅 at **55% WITHOUT** this trim, and flagged that a go-last
   disadvantage is **under-counted in sim** — so this lost turn could **over-correct him below 45%**. Because
   it changes win rate it was **deliberately not re-simmed** — it's being tested here. *Instrument:* if 網紅
   is in the game, track his win/loss and whether the lost night-turn felt fair or crippling; ask the 網紅
   player if the missed turn hurt. **Feed the DIAL (below).**

## 4. The decisions this session feeds back
- **−½ penalty (fork #2):** keep if players agonise over claims / speculate; cut if nobody does.
- **Night sprint (Probe 7):** shorten the night half if it overstays after dusk.
- **Disruptor teeth (Probe 9):** add contest-reward if the lane feels toothless.
- **網紅 (the CD's lost-turn flaw — §2.8):** dial from the session result. **Default =** misses first turn
  of the **night** phase (locked). **Too strong (>55%) →** heavier: misses first turn of **BOTH** phases, or
  forfeits his **final move** at the last train. **Over-corrected (<45%) →** revert toward **keep+watch**
  (drop the lost turn, keep goes-last + the ability, and instead trim the ability: +1 Buzz on your *first*
  Promote only, or a small bribe tab).
- **Central (Probe 4):** confirm the tea-export data tweak if Central feels dead.
- **Weight/parse (component load):** `bg-elegance` + `bg-infodesign` confirm the table stays readable.

## 5. The open sensitivity gate (carry from bg-theme — MUST clear before art)
The **East apex** is a placeholder — **`部落風味 "Tribal Signature"` is NOT shippable.** It must become a
**specific, real, respectfully-named Indigenous dish**. **Recommendation: `石板烤肉` (stone-slab BBQ)** — a
widely-known, celebrated dish across several Taiwanese Indigenous communities, concrete rather than a
generic "tribal" label. **Bind:** a **paid Taiwanese / Indigenous cultural + language consultant signs off
on the specific dish, its naming (繁體 + the appropriate romanisation), and its framing BEFORE any art or
print.** The East region's Indigenous-forward identity carries the studio's standing sensitivity rails
(concrete not generic, dignity, secular, consultant-gated). This is the #1 pre-art consultant item alongside
the Bribe/大哥 warmth check.
