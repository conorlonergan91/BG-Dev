# v6.2 — Screentop setup note (stand up the 2p module)

> Written by `bg-prototyper` for the first v6.2 2-player human playtest (the session in
> `v6.2-screentop-plan.md`). Everything here is **regenerated from data** by `./make.sh`; the
> play-ready upload set lives in **`exports/screentop/`** (one sheet + one back per pile). The overview
> PnP sheets, tracks, board, mats, cover, rulebook and player aid live at the top of **`exports/`**.
> Theme: **night-market**. Card cells are 409×585 px; every deck sheet is within Screentop's ≤10×7,
> ≤4096 px limits.

## The day/night read (so the table can see the clock at a glance)
Cards are recoloured by their `phase`: **DAY = warm gold frame + a ☀ sun badge + "日 DAY 早" ribbon**;
**NIGHT = neon-violet frame + a ☾ crescent badge + "夜 NIGHT 市" ribbon** (dusk ground drifting to
lantern lavender); **general tickets = gold "通用 ANY" ribbon**. Region colour still carries *where*
(North vermilion△ · Central teal◆ · South amber● · East indigo■); phase carries *when*. All badges are
drawn shapes (not font glyphs), so nothing renders as a tofu box.

## How to import a deck in Screentop
New Game → add a **Deck** → upload the `*_sheet_1.png` as the face, set **Columns × Rows** to the grid
in the tables below, and set the deck **Back** to the matching `*_back.png`. The last card-art cell of
each face sheet is the concentric-ring back (harmless — you're overriding it with the standalone back);
a couple of decks have **one trailing blank cell** — delete that single blank card after import. Then
**shuffle** each deck. Share the room link (`v6.2-screentop-plan.md` §1 for who plays).

## 2. The 8 VENDOR decks (per region + phase) — with the face-up markets
Set each region's **DAY vendor deck** as a face-down pile; flip its **face-up** market row (**North 2,
others 1** at 2p). Refill the face-up slot in place from that region's deck when a vendor is bribed/removed.
Hold the **NIGHT vendor decks** aside; at dusk clear the day vendors island-wide and deal the night ones.

| Region | DAY deck | face-up | NIGHT deck | face-up |
|---|---|:--:|---|:--:|
| North 北 | `vend_north-day_sheet_1.png` (4×1, 3) | **2** | `vend_north-night_sheet_1.png` (5×1, 4) | **2** |
| Central 中 | `vend_central-day_sheet_1.png` (4×1, 3) | 1 | `vend_central-night_sheet_1.png` (4×1, 3) | 1 |

So **5 vendors are courtable at once** in each phase. Each vendor's apex ★ (one per region, night) prints
its Prestige/Rep/perk; every vendor prints the ≥2 Buzz majority (大哥's ≥1 is a mat-level override).
