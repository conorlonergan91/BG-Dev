## Concept & premise
A lighthearted travel-and-food game set in **Taiwan**. Players are "travel agents" (with a playful
secret-agent wink) on a mission to have the best adventure — chasing night-market snacks, riding the
rail, and discovering the island's food & culture. The comedy is warm and celebratory of Taiwanese food
culture, not mocking.

## Target aesthetics (the kinds of fun)
<!-- Pick 2–3. -->
Candidates: **Discovery** (uncovering places & dishes), **Fellowship** (shared table joy / friendly
rivalry), **Challenge** (optimise your trip). To confirm with `bg-theme`.

## Tone & vibe
Lighthearted, playful, warm, adventurous — quirky humour throughout. One tone, held across art, names,
and rules-voice. The secret-agent framing is a **wink**, not a spy-thriller.

## Restraint rung
Likely **evocative theme** (rich, felt setting; light narrative) — not a deep lore world. Setting does
the heavy lifting; humour is the seasoning.

## Story / world / lore
Light by design. The "mission" framing is comedic scaffolding; the real story is the trip you take.

## Theme ↔ mechanic resonance
<!-- To be developed as the spine is chosen — the win state should FEEL like "the best trip". -->
Aim: a player could half-guess the rules from the fiction (collect dishes = eat; build routes = travel;
missions = the agent gag). Watch that the "agent" layer earns a rule or it's just paint.
