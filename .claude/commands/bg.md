give a 3-line "where we are / what's next" recap, and propose the next stage as an A/B/C choice.

blank slate, (B) develop an idea they bring, or (C) tour the studio.

User's focus for this session (may be empty): $ARGUMENTS

Keep it fun, keep the human in the loop, and keep decisions quick (A/B/C with a recommendation).
